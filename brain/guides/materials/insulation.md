> **Guide, draft.** The R-values a house needs come from its energy rating
> report, or from the tables in Housing Provisions Part 13.2 for its
> climate zone and construction. Build to the stamped report.

# R-values

**R-value** is resistance to heat flow: higher is better. Two numbers to
keep apart:

- **Product R-value**: printed on the pack (e.g. R2.5 wall batts, R6.0 ceiling batts).
- **Total R-value**: the whole wall, roof or floor, including linings, air gaps and reflective layers. The NCC's tables and the energy rating use the total, so a product R-value isn't automatically the total.

# Types

| Type | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Glasswool batts** | Walls, ceilings, floors | Most common; wear PPE when cutting and fitting |
| **Polyester batts** | Walls, ceilings | No itch; often dearer per R-value |
| **Rockwool (stone wool)** | Walls, acoustic, near heat | Denser; good for sound and fire |
| **Reflective foil and foil blankets** | Roofs (under sheet metal), walls | Needs an airspace next to the foil to work; often doubles as sarking |
| **Rigid boards (PIR, XPS, EPS)** | Slab edges, under slabs, external walls, cathedral ceilings | High R per mm; check fire and termite requirements |

Batt sizes are made to fit between studs and joists at standard spacing
(for example 430 mm and 580 mm widths for 450 and 600 mm centres); check
the framing before ordering.

# Installing to the NCC (13.2.2)

- Insulation complies with **AS/NZS 4859.1**.
- **No gaps**: butt or overlap adjoining insulation, and butt it tight against studs, noggings and joists.
- **Don't squash it**: bulk insulation has to keep its thickness, except where it crosses battens, pipes and cables. A compressed R2.5 batt isn't R2.5.
- Continuous with ceilings, walls and floors; in a ceiling over an uninsulated wall, overlap the wall by at least 50 mm.
- Reflective foil: the right airspace, fitted close at openings, supported by framing, and joins lapped at least 150 mm or taped.
- Keep the clearances that downlights, flues and electrical equipment need (the fitting's instructions and AS/NZS 3000 for electrics).

# Sealing (13.4)

Insulation works only if air can't blow through: seal around windows and
doors, fit dampers to exhaust fans, and seal penetrations, in the rooms and
climate zones Part 13.4 covers. See [energy efficiency](../code/energy_efficiency.md)
and [sarking and wraps](sarking_and_wraps.md).

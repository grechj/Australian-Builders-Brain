> **Plain-English guide, draft.** It summarises the deemed-to-satisfy
> provisions for houses. The NCC text wins: follow the clause links, check
> your state's variations (SA replaces 10.2.1 and adds a floor waste
> clause), and use the edition in force when the permit application was
> lodged. Most states also license waterproofers or require a certificate:
> check your state page.

Part 10.2 ([2025](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/10_2_wet_area_waterproofing.md#10_2_1), [2022](/ncc/2022/housing_provisions/10_2_wet_area_waterproofing.md#10_2_1)) uses two levels:

- **Waterproof:** a proper waterproofing system, such as a membrane to AS/NZS 4858, stainless steel, flexible waterproof sheet flooring with waterproof joints, or waterproof sealant (10.2.8).
- **Water resistant:** a moisture-tolerant substrate (10.2.9), such as fibre-cement sheet, water-resistant plasterboard, concrete or treated render or masonry, with a water-resistant surface over it (10.2.10), such as tiles, laminate or acrylic linings.

# What goes where

| Area | Floor | Walls | Junctions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shower** (enclosed or not) | Waterproof, including the hob or step-down | Waterproof to **1800 mm** above the floor substrate | Wall-wall, wall-floor and penetrations waterproof |
| **Rest of the bathroom** | Water resistant on concrete or fibre-cement; **waterproof on timber, ply or particleboard** | — | Wall-floor waterproof; a flashing needs a horizontal leg of at least 40 mm |
| **Next to a bath or spa** | As for the rest of the room | Water resistant 150 mm above the bath where it's within 75 mm of a wall | Wall-floor waterproof along the bath |
| **Inserted bath or spa** | Shelf around it waterproof, with a waterstop under the lip | Waterproof 150 mm above the lip | Waterproof within 150 mm |
| **Basin, sink, tub** | — | Water resistant 150 mm above it, where it's within 75 mm of a wall | Waterproof where it's fixed to the wall |
| **Laundry, WC** | Water resistant | — | Wall-floor water resistant (40 mm flashing leg) |
| **WC with a hand-held bidet spray** | Waterproof | Within 900 mm of the spray: waterproof to 150 mm, water resistant to 1200 mm | Waterproof |

Clauses 10.2.2 to 10.2.5. With a preformed shower base, the floor rule
doesn't apply but the walls, junctions and penetrations still do.

# Falls and shower set-downs

- Where there's a floor waste, falls to it between **1:80 and 1:50** (10.2.12).
- Every shower needs a floor waste with those falls, and an enclosed shower needs a step-down, hob, level threshold or preformed base built to 10.2.15 to 10.2.19 (10.2.14).

# Changed in NCC 2025

NCC 2022 required shower wall junctions to be waterproof **40 mm either
side** of the junction. NCC 2025 says the junctions must be waterproof
without that figure. The 1800 mm shower wall height is the same in both.

# On site

- Membrane to AS/NZS 4858, applied to the manufacturer's system (primer, bond breakers at junctions, number of coats, cure times). The manufacturer's system is how the junction and penetration details are met in practice.
- Photograph the membrane before tiling, with the flood test if you do one: it's your evidence if a leak comes later. Use the [quality hold points](/playbooks/quality_hold_points.md) before covering up.
- Use wet-area board (water-resistant plasterboard or fibre-cement) as the wall substrate. See [plasterboard and lining boards](../materials/plasterboard_and_linings.md) and [membranes and sealants](../materials/membranes_and_sealants.md).

> **Plain-English guide, draft.** The NCC text wins: follow the clause
> links. Use the edition in force when the permit application was lodged.

Clause 10.3.1 ([2025](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/10_3_room_heights.md#10_3_1), [2022](/ncc/2022/housing_provisions/10_3_room_heights.md#10_3_1)). Measure to the finished ceiling.

| Room or space | Minimum height |
|---|---|
| Living rooms, bedrooms and other habitable rooms (not kitchens) | 2.4 m |
| Kitchen | 2.1 m |
| Hallways and passages | 2.1 m |
| Bathroom, laundry, WC, pantry, storeroom, garage | 2.1 m |
| Stair, ramp or landing | 2.0 m above the nosing line or ramp floor |

**Sloping ceilings and bulkheads:** the minimum only has to be met over
**two-thirds of the floor area**: 2.4 m in a habitable room, 2.2 m in an
attic room, 2.1 m in a non-habitable room. Leave out any part lower than
1.5 m when working out the floor area.

A worked check: an attic bedroom of 12 m² with 3 m² under 1.5 m has 9 m²
that counts, so at least 6 m² of it needs 2.2 m of headroom.

# On site

- Check heights on the frame before lining, especially under bulkheads for ducts and over stairs in renovations.
- NCC 2025 excludes threshold ramps from the 2.0 m stair and ramp rule; NCC 2022 doesn't mention them.

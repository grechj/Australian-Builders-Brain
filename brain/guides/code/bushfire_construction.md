> **Plain-English guide, draft.** The NCC sends bushfire construction to
> AS 3959 (or the NASH steel-framing standard), which isn't reproduced here:
> get the standard, or the manufacturer's BAL-rated details, for the actual
> construction requirements. Use the edition in force when the permit
> application was lodged.

H7D4 ([2025](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h7_ancillary_provisions_and_additional_construction_requirements.md#h7d4), [2022](/ncc/2022/volume_two/h7_ancillary_provisions_and_additional_construction_requirements.md#h7d4)).

# When it applies

Only in a **designated bushfire prone area**: land your state or council
has mapped as bushfire prone. Check the planning maps or ask the certifier
before quoting. When it applies, it covers:

- the house (Class 1),
- a garage, carport or shed that's a Class 10a building, and
- **a deck associated with the house**.

So a new deck on a house in a mapped area is bushfire construction too,
even if the house was built before the mapping.

# How it works: the BAL

The site gets a **bushfire attack level (BAL)** from an assessment under
AS 3959, based on the vegetation, its distance and the slope. The levels
run BAL-LOW, BAL-12.5, BAL-19, BAL-29, BAL-40 and BAL-FZ (flame zone). The
higher the BAL, the stricter the requirements for:

- external walls and cladding, and sarking
- windows, glazing, frames and screens (ember and radiant heat)
- external doors and door frames
- roofs, gutters, vents and gaps (ember guards, no gaps over a set size)
- decks, verandahs, steps and their supporting posts and decking boards
- subfloor enclosure
- water and gas supply pipes above ground

The building complies by meeting AS 3959 for its BAL, or the NASH
standard for steel framing. Most suppliers publish which BAL their
products are tested or rated to: ask before you buy, and keep the
certificates for the certifier.

# State variations

- **NSW** replaces H7D4: AS 3959 or the NASH standard apply **as amended by Planning for Bush Fire Protection**, or as modified by the development consent or a bushfire safety authority under the Rural Fires Act. Read the DA conditions first.
- **QLD** adds H7D4(3): the requirements don't apply where the classified vegetation is Group F rainforest (not wet sclerophyll), mangroves, or grassland under 300 mm high.
- **SA** adds H7D4(3): the BAL depends on the Planning and Design Code area (general risk is BAL-LOW, medium risk BAL-12.5, high risk assessed to AS 3959, with rules for urban interface areas).
- **TAS** and **NSW** also replace the performance requirement H7P5. Check the clause page for your state.

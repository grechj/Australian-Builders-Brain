> **Plain-English guide, draft.** Part 4.2 only applies within the limits in
> H1D4(2) (site class, building size and the like); outside them, footings
> are engineered to AS 2870. Most new houses have an engineer's footing
> design anyway: the engineer's drawings win over this guide. Use the
> edition in force when the permit application was lodged.

Part 4.2 ([2025](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/4_2_footings_slabs_and_associated_elements.md#4_2_1), [2022](/ncc/2022/housing_provisions/4_2_footings_slabs_and_associated_elements.md#4_2_1)).

# Before you dig

- **Site classification** (4.2.2): the site is classified under AS 2870 from a soil test (Class A, S, M, H1, H2, E or P). It drives every footing size, so get the report before pricing.
- **Excavation** (4.2.3): clean, vertical sides where possible; base level (up to 1:40 to drain) or no steeper than 1:10 on a slope; no loose earth, roots or mud; topsoil off. On loose sand or erosion-prone sites, footings at least **300 mm** below finished ground.

# Fill and bearing

- **Fill under a slab** (4.2.4): controlled sand fill up to 800 mm (300 mm layers, vibrating plate or roller) or clay up to 400 mm (150 mm layers, roller); rolled fill shallower. Then at least 20 mm of clean quarry sand on top.
- **Bearing** (4.2.5): slab panels and edge beams tied to the slab on natural ground of at least **50 kPa** (or proper fill); pads, strips and edge beams not tied to the slab on natural ground of at least **100 kPa**.

# Slab details

- **Vapour barrier** (4.2.8): under every house slab, **0.2 mm** polyethylene, medium impact resistance, branded to AS 2870; laps at least **200 mm**; taped or sleeved at penetrations; punctures patched; carried under the beams to ground level.
- **Concrete** (4.2.10): at least **N20** (20 MPa at 28 days), 20 mm aggregate, 100 mm slump. **No water added on site** to loosen it up.
- **Reinforcement** (4.2.11): mesh, trench mesh or bars to AS 2870, clean, tied and on chairs (no more than 800 mm apart for mesh). Re-entrant corners get diagonal trench mesh or bars.
- **Cover** to the steel: 40 mm to ground, 30 mm to a membrane, 20 mm internal, 40 mm to the weather.
- **Edge rebates** for brick veneer (4.2.9): at least 20 mm, flashed and drained.

# Stumps for decks and suspended floors (4.2.13)

- Size the stumps and pads from the tables in 4.2.13 for Class A and S sites, otherwise from AS 1684, AS 3600, AS 4100 or the engineer.
- Embed stumps at least **30% of their height above ground, or 450 mm**, whichever is more.
- Concrete stumps at least 20 MPa. Steel stumps sealed with a welded top plate, set in concrete finished at least **100 mm above ground** and sloped away, and corrosion protected.
- Brace the stumps (perimeter masonry, or to the material's standard).

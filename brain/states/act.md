# Rules and licensing

- **NCC 2025 has no ACT clause variations.** The ACT's variations and additions are in the ACT Appendix to the ACT Building Code, published under the Building Act 2004, separately from the NCC. Check it for ACT jobs.
- Access Canberra licenses builders and trades, and building certifiers manage approvals and inspections.

# Climate and materials

- H2 or H2F treated framing is typical.
- Bushfire-prone areas: check the BAL rating.
- The NCC climate zone for the site (zones 1 to 8) drives the energy-efficiency requirements. Check the zone for the postcode.

# Costs and suppliers

- Canberra prices are generally close to Sydney's.
- For pricing, use the [materials](/contracts/materials_estimating.md) location loading to allow for freight or remoteness, using a figure from local suppliers rather than a guess.
- Trade-specific suppliers are listed on each [trade profile](/trades/index.md). Search for the ones serving the job's area.

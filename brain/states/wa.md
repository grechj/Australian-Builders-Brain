# Rules and licensing

- WA adds a water-use Part (H9) and changes to AS/NZS 3500.3 (H10) to NCC Volume Two, and changes to AS/NZS 3500.3 for Volume One buildings.
- Building and Energy registers builders and licenses plumbers, gasfitters and electricians.

# Climate and materials

- Northern WA (Pilbara, Kimberley) is cyclonic (wind regions C and D): use cyclone-rated fixings, roofing and windows.
- Double brick is common in Perth; masonry trades and materials dominate residential work.
- North of the Tropic of Capricorn, termite-treated framing is H2S.
- The NCC climate zone for the site (zones 1 to 8) drives the energy-efficiency requirements. Check the zone for the postcode.

# Costs and suppliers

- Regional and remote WA prices can be far above Perth: freight and limited supply. Price locally.
- For pricing, use the [materials](/contracts/materials_estimating.md) location loading to allow for freight or remoteness, using a figure from local suppliers rather than a guess.
- Trade-specific suppliers are listed on each [trade profile](/trades/index.md). Search for the ones serving the job's area.

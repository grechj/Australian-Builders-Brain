# Rules and licensing

- QBCC licenses builders and most trades (including plumbers, drainers and waterproofers). Electricians are licensed by the Electrical Safety Office.
- Home warranty is through the QBCC statutory insurance scheme above the threshold.

# Climate and materials

- **Cyclonic regions.** Coastal north Queensland is in cyclonic wind regions C and D: tie-downs, fixings, roofing and windows must be rated for the site's wind classification, and NCC Specification 4 (design of buildings in cyclonic areas) applies to Volume One buildings.
- North of the Tropic of Capricorn, termite-treated framing is H2S rather than the H2F sold in the south.
- High termite hazard: termite management (AS 3660) is required throughout.
- The NCC climate zone for the site (zones 1 to 8) drives the energy-efficiency requirements. Check the zone for the postcode.

# Costs and suppliers

- Remote and far-north jobs carry significant freight loadings, and cyclone-rated products cost more. Get local supplier prices for those areas.
- For pricing, use the [materials](/contracts/materials_estimating.md) location loading to allow for freight or remoteness, using a figure from local suppliers rather than a guess.
- Trade-specific suppliers are listed on each [trade profile](/trades/index.md). Search for the ones serving the job's area.

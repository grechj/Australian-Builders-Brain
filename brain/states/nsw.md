# Rules and licensing

- Check a licence on the register above, and an ABN on ABN Lookup: see [checking ABNs, licences and insurance](/playbooks/checking_registers.md).
- **Energy efficiency: BASIX.** For houses, NSW replaces the NCC's energy-efficiency Part (H6) with BASIX. The BASIX certificate issued with the approval sets the insulation, glazing, water and energy commitments.
- Approvals are a development application (DA) or complying development certificate (CDC), then a construction certificate. Home building compensation (HBCF) insurance is needed above the threshold.
- Trade licences (builder, electrician, plumber, gasfitter and others) are issued by NSW Fair Trading.

# Climate and materials

- Termite-treated framing for most of the state is H2 or H2F ("blue" pine, for south of the Tropic of Capricorn).
- Bushfire-prone land is common: check the BAL rating, which drives AS 3959 construction requirements and materials.
- Alpine areas (Snowy Mountains) have extra NCC provisions.
- The NCC climate zone for the site (zones 1 to 8) drives the energy-efficiency requirements. Check the zone for the postcode.

# Costs and suppliers

- Sydney metro prices are the usual reference; regional NSW often adds freight on bulky items (timber, steel, concrete).
- For pricing, use the [materials](/contracts/materials_estimating.md) location loading to allow for freight or remoteness, using a figure from local suppliers rather than a guess.
- Trade-specific suppliers are listed on each [trade profile](/trades/index.md). Search for the ones serving the job's area.

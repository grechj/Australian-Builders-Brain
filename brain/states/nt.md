# Rules and licensing

- The Building Practitioners Board registers builders and certifiers.

# Climate and materials

- **Cyclonic regions.** Darwin and the Top End are in cyclonic wind region C: cyclone-rated tie-downs, fixings, roofing and windows are required.
- Termite-treated framing is H2S (north of the Tropic of Capricorn), and termite hazard is high.
- Heat, humidity and the wet season affect concrete, finishes and scheduling.
- The NCC climate zone for the site (zones 1 to 8) drives the energy-efficiency requirements. Check the zone for the postcode.

# Costs and suppliers

- Expect substantial freight loadings in Darwin and much more for remote communities. Price with local suppliers.
- For pricing, use the [materials](/contracts/materials_estimating.md) location loading to allow for freight or remoteness, using a figure from local suppliers rather than a guess.
- Trade-specific suppliers are listed on each [trade profile](/trades/index.md). Search for the ones serving the job's area.

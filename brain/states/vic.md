# Rules and licensing

- Check a licence on the register above, and an ABN on ABN Lookup: see [checking ABNs, licences and insurance](/playbooks/checking_registers.md).
- Victoria's plumbing regulations add Parts to NCC Volume Three for HVAC, stormwater roof drainage, surface drainage and low-risk trade waste. They apply to plumbers, roofers (roof drainage) and HVAC installers.
- Builders and plumbers are registered or licensed by the Building and Plumbing Commission; electricians are licensed by Energy Safe Victoria.
- Domestic building insurance is needed above the threshold.

# Climate and materials

- H2 or H2F treated framing is typical; Victoria has termite-free areas where councils don't require termite management, so check the council.
- Bushfire-prone areas are widespread: check the BAL rating.
- Alpine areas have extra NCC provisions.
- The NCC climate zone for the site (zones 1 to 8) drives the energy-efficiency requirements. Check the zone for the postcode.

# Costs and suppliers

- Melbourne metro is the usual price reference; regional areas may add freight.
- For pricing, use the [materials](/contracts/materials_estimating.md) location loading to allow for freight or remoteness, using a figure from local suppliers rather than a guess.
- Trade-specific suppliers are listed on each [trade profile](/trades/index.md). Search for the ones serving the job's area.

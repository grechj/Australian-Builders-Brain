The Australian Builder's Brain: knowledge for Australian builders and
tradespeople, from quote to final account. Start with the job's
[lifecycle stage](lifecycle/), then follow links. Concepts follow the
[Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format).

# Folders

* [Computations](computations/) - Attested money calculations: GST, variation price, margin
* [Contracts](contracts/) - Contract topics: variations, extensions of time, HIA/MBA clause map
* [Documents](documents/) - One page per template: what it's for, its fields, which skill fills it
* [Fields](fields/) - The shared project fields every template fills from
* [Glossary](glossary/) - Site slang and trade terms
* [Lifecycle](lifecycle/) - The five stages of a job, from quote to final account, with the documents used at each
* [NCC](ncc/) - National Construction Code: editions, volumes, how to cite
* [Phases](phases/) - Construction phases by project type: activities, inspections, materials, weather risk, next trades
* [Playbooks](playbooks/) - Step-by-step checklists: PC inspection, claim support pack, pre-construction, hold points, safety inspection
* [References](references/) - Playbooks, sanctioned code and attesters that other concepts point to
* [States](states/) - State and territory rules: regulators, home building and security-of-payment laws

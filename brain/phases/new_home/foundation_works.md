Typical timing: days 11 to 25 from site start (a rough guide from BEAM's phase engine, not a program).[^beam_phase_engine]

# Typical activities
- Site excavation & levelling
- Footings trenches
- Termite treatment
- Compaction testing
- Reo / steel fixing
- Formwork installation
- Underground drainage
- Under-slab plumbing rough-in
- Concrete pour — footings
- Waterproofing (sub-slab)
- Slab preparation & pour
- Strip forms / saw cuts / curing

# Inspection hold point
- **Slab / footings inspection**: Before concrete pour.

Your certifier or council sets the inspection stages that actually apply. Check the approval conditions.

# Ready for tomorrow?
- Concrete booking confirmed?
- Reo inspected / signed off?
- Formwork complete & checked?
- Weather suitable for pour?

# Key materials to track
- Concrete
- Reinforcement / reo
- Formwork
- Aggregate / fill

# Weather risk
- Concrete pours may need rescheduling
- Excavation unsafe in wet conditions
- Formwork at risk of water ingress

Record lost days in the [site diary](/documents/site_diary.md). They may support an [extension of time](/contracts/extension_of_time.md).

# Trades that usually follow
Carpenter, steel fixer. See [trade sequence](/phases/trade_sequence.md).

Typical timing: days 26 to 45 from site start (a rough guide, not a program).[^beam_phase_engine]

# Typical activities
- Frame delivery
- Floor joists / bearers install
- Wall frames assembly & erect
- Structural steel (if required)
- Truss / rafter install
- Roof bracing
- Upper floor structure (if 2-storey)
- Stairs installation
- Fascia & eaves
- Frame inspection
- Window frames

# Inspection hold point
- **Frame inspection**: Before cladding / lining.

Your certifier or council sets the inspection stages that actually apply. Check the approval conditions.

# Ready for tomorrow?
- Frame materials on site?
- Trusses delivered / scheduled?
- Frame inspection booked?
- Bracing & tie-down checked?

# Key materials to track
- Wall frames / timber
- Trusses / rafters
- Structural steel
- Bracing / tie-downs

# Weather risk
- Timber framing shouldn't be erected in high winds
- Crane lifts may be cancelled

Record lost days in the [site diary](/documents/site_diary.md). They may support an [extension of time](/contracts/extension_of_time.md).

# Trades that usually follow
Roofer, bricklayer. See [trade sequence](/phases/trade_sequence.md).

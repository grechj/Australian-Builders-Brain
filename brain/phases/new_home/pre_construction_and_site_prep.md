# Typical activities
- Site evaluation / soil testing
- Building permits & approvals
- Site clearing & demolition
- Temporary fencing & signage
- Portaloo / amenities delivery
- Erosion & sediment control
- Set out / survey
- Environmental protection measures

# Ready for tomorrow?
- Permits confirmed for next stage?
- Site access clear for trades?
- Temporary services connected?

# Weather risk
- Earthworks may be delayed
- Erosion controls under pressure

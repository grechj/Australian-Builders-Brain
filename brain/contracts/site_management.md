Good site records win extension of time claims, defend variations, and
settle disputes. Most builders lose arguments because they can't prove what
happened, not because they were wrong.

# Site diary

Fill in the [Site Diary](/documents/site_diary.md) every day the site is
active, and on days it isn't (rain, waiting on others):

- Weather, morning and afternoon, from the dropdown. The Weather Summary counts rain days for [EOT claims](/contracts/extension_of_time.md). Keep the BOM daily records for the nearest station as backup.
- Trades on site, work completed, delays and their causes, instructions given, and visitors (inspectors, engineer, client)
- Write it on the day. A diary written later is easy to attack.

Every delay entry should say what was delayed and why, so it can support an
EOT. Every verbal instruction should become a
[site instruction](/contracts/site_instructions_and_back_charges.md).

# Client progress updates

A short weekly [Progress Update](/documents/progress_update_email.md)
heads off most client disputes:

- Work done this week and planned for next week
- The current stage, % complete, and whether the job is on program (and why not)
- Decisions or selections needed from the client, **with a date**. Late selections are a common cause of delay and should be documented.
- Pending variations, and when the next claim is expected

Work out the week number with [site week](/computations/site_week.md), and
take % complete from the latest [progress claim](/computations/progress_claim.md)
(claimed to date ÷ schedule total).

# Materials and trades

- Log every order in the [Material Order Tracker](/documents/material_order_tracker.md): supplier, PO, delivery date and whether it arrived. Long-lead items (windows, trusses, joinery) get ordered early.
- Book trades with their lead times in the [Trade Booking and Lead Time Tracker](/documents/trade_booking_lead_time_tracker.md). Check the next trade is ready using the [trade sequence](/phases/trade_sequence.md) and the readiness checks on each [construction phase](/phases/index.md).

# Quality hold points

Sign off each [quality hold point](/playbooks/quality_hold_points.md)
before the work is covered up, and record it in the diary. The certifier's
mandatory inspections are hold points too: book them early, and don't
cover work before they pass.

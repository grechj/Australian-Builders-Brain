Walk the site with this list at least weekly, and after any change in
conditions (new high-risk work, after storms).[^beam_safety_inspection]
Fix or isolate anything unsafe before work continues, record the
inspection in the [site diary](/documents/site_diary.md), and raise
recurring issues at the next [toolbox talk](/documents/toolbox_talk_record.md).
See [work health and safety](/contracts/work_health_and_safety.md).

## General Site Conditions

- [ ] Site fencing and hoarding secure and in good condition
- [ ] Site signage displayed (builder details, safety signs)
- [ ] Access and egress routes clear and unobstructed
- [ ] Housekeeping — site tidy, materials stored safely
- [ ] Waste managed — skip bins not overflowing, sorted correctly
- [ ] Amenities clean and functional (toilet, hand wash, lunch area)

## Personal Protective Equipment

- [ ] All workers wearing hard hats in required areas
- [ ] High-visibility clothing worn by all personnel
- [ ] Safety footwear (steel cap boots) worn by all
- [ ] Eye protection available and used where required
- [ ] Hearing protection available near noisy work
- [ ] Gloves and other task-specific PPE available

## Fall Prevention

- [ ] Edge protection installed on open edges > 2m
- [ ] Scaffolding erected by competent person, tagged current
- [ ] Scaffold boards secure, no gaps, kickboards installed
- [ ] Ladders in good condition, secured at top
- [ ] Penetrations and openings covered or barricaded
- [ ] Safety mesh / catch platforms installed (if required)

## Electrical Safety

- [ ] RCDs (safety switches) installed and tested
- [ ] All leads and tools tested and tagged (current)
- [ ] No damaged leads, plugs, or connections
- [ ] Temporary power board in good condition
- [ ] Overhead power line exclusion zones maintained

## Excavation & Earthworks

- [ ] Excavations benched, battered, or shored (if > 1.5m)
- [ ] Barriers around open excavations
- [ ] Services located and marked (Before You Dig Australia, BYDA)
- [ ] Soil stockpiles stable and clear of edge

## Fire Prevention

- [ ] Fire extinguisher on site, serviced and accessible
- [ ] Hot work permits in place (welding, cutting, grinding)
- [ ] Flammable materials stored safely
- [ ] No smoking in prohibited areas

## Emergency Preparedness

- [ ] First aid kit stocked and accessible
- [ ] Emergency contact list displayed
- [ ] Emergency evacuation plan and assembly point known
- [ ] Trained first aider on site

# Concepts

* [Claim support pack](claim_support_pack.md) - What to attach to a progress claim so it gets paid, from the BEAM claim support pack checklist.
* [Practical completion inspection](practical_completion_inspection.md) - Room-by-room checklist to work through before issuing a practical completion notice, from the BEAM PC checklist.
* [Pre-construction checklist](pre_construction.md) - Everything to have in place before breaking ground (contracts, approvals, insurance, documents, site set-up and safety), from the BEAM pre-construction checklist.
* [Quality hold points](quality_hold_points.md) - Checks to sign off at each stage before the work is covered up: slab, frame, pre-lining, fixing and PC, with who checks each one.
* [Site safety inspection](site_safety_inspection.md) - Weekly site safety walk-through: site conditions, PPE, fall prevention, electrical, excavation, fire and emergency preparedness.

### VIC C2P8 No point of connection

*Insert VIC C2P8 as follows:*

[New for 2022] Where a *point of connection* to a *Network Utility Operator’s* sewerage system is not available, an *on-site wastewater* *management system* must be designed, installed and maintained in accordance with the requirements and agreement of the relevant authority having jurisdiction.

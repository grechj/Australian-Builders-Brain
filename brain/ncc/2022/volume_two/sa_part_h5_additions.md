> **NCC 2022 as published (1 May 2023).** For work under NCC 2022 it must be read with Amendment 1 (from 1 May 2025) and Amendment 2 (from 29 July 2025), which change some clauses. See [NCC editions](/ncc/editions.md).

Clauses that apply only in SA, from the SA schedule of **NCC 2022 Volume Two**.[^abcb_ncc]

<a id="sa_h5o2"></a>

### SA H5O2 Objective

*Insert SA H5O2 as follows:*

The Objective is to safeguard people from injury and illness resulting from the creation of hazardous spaces between buildings.

<a id="sa_h5f2"></a>

### SA H5F2 Space between buildings

*Insert SA H5F2 as follows:*

The space between buildings must not allow hazardous conditions to arise due to accumulation of rubbish that cannot be easily removed.

<a id="sa_h5p3"></a>

### SA H5P3 Access for inspection and maintenance

*Insert SA H5P3 as follows:*

The space between buildings must be sufficient to allow access for inspection and maintenance to avoid hazardous conditions arising due to accumulation of rubbish that could—

- (a) bridge termite barriers; or
- (b) harbour vermin; or
- (c) create a fire hazard.
<a id="sa_h5d4"></a>

### SA H5D4 Minimum space between buildings

*Insert SA H5D4 as follows:*

(1) Compliance with (2) for acceptable separation between buildings for Class 1 and 10 buildings satisfies *Performance* *Requirement* SA H5P3.

(2) Unless the space between external columns is not infilled, every part of an *external wall* of a building must be not less than 600 mm from—

- (a) any boundary of the allotment, unless that wall is on or abutting that boundary; or
- (b) the *external wall* of any other building on the same allotment, unless the two buildings are abutting.

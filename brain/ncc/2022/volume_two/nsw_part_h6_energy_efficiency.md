### NSW H6P3 Services

*Domestic services*, including any associated distribution system and components must, to the degree necessary, have features that facilitate the efficient use of energy appropriate to—

- (a) the *domestic service* and its usage; and
- (b) the geographic location of the building; and
- (c) the location of the *domestic service*; and
- (d) the energy source. Limitations NSW H6P3 does not apply to existing services associated with existing buildings being relocated. Verification Methods

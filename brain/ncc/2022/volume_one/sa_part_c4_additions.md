### SA C4D18 Protection of openings – bulk grain storage facilities

*Insert SA C4D18 as follows:*

Where external stairs, ramps or ladders are used as an *exit* in a *bulk grain storage facility*, any window or door opening within 6 m of the stairway or ladder—

- (a) must be protected in accordance with C4D5; or
- (b) the stairway, ramp or ladder must be enclosed for its full height above the lowest level of the window or door opening with *non-combustible* construction having an FRL of not less than 60/60/60.

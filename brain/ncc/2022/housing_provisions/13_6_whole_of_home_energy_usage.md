### 13.6.1 Application of Part 13.6

> *NSW Part 13.6 applies here: see the state variation below.*

> *NT Part 13.6 applies here: see the state variation below.*

> *TAS Part 13.6 applies here: see the state variation below.*

[New for 2022]

(1) This Part applies to—

- (a) a Class 1 building; and
- (b) a Class 10a building with a *conditioned space*.
(2) Part 13.6 must be applied as directed in H6D2(2).

### 13.6.2 Net equivalent energy usage

[New for 2022]

(1) The net equivalent energy usage of a building, calculated in accordance with (a), must not exceed the allowance calculated in accordance with (b)—

- (a) (cid:1)A (cid:2)EE(cid:2) (cid:1)EP(cid:1)ES (cid:3)ER, where—
  - (i) A= the floor area factor obtained by multiplying the total floor area of the building by the adjustment factor in Table 13.6.2a; and
  - (ii) E = the *main space conditioning* and *main water heater* efficiency factor obtained from the ABCB Standard for Whole-of-Home Efficiency Factors; and
  - (iii) EP= the swimming pool pump energy usage in (2); and
  - (iv) E = the spa pump energy usage in (3); and
  - (v) E = the installed capacity of on-site photovoltaics (kW); and
- (b) A (cid:1)E , where—
  - (i) A= the floor area factor obtained from multiplying the total floor area of the building by the adjustment factor in Table 13.6.2a; and
  - (ii) E = the energy factor obtained from in Table 13.6.2b.
(2) The swimming pool pump energy usage (E ) must be determined in accordance with the following formula: E (cid:2) V (cid:3)F (cid:1)1000 , where—

- (a) EP= the swimming pool pump energy usage; and
- (b) V= the volume of the swimming pool to the nearest 1000 litres; and
- (c) F = the swimming pool pump factor as per Table 13.6.2c.
(3) The spa pump energy usage (E ) must be determined in accordance with the following formula: E (cid:2) V (cid:3)F (cid:1)100 , where—

- (a) E = the spa pump energy usage; and
- (b) V= the volume of the spa to the nearest 100 litres; and
- (c) F = the spa pump factor as per Table 13.6.2d.

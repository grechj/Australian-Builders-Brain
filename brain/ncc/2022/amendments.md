NCC 2022 as published (1 May 2023) has been amended twice. The 2022 pages in this brain keep the published text and show each amendment as a notice under the clause it changes, so you can see both what changed and when.

# NCC 2022 Amendment 1

- **In force from:** 1 May 2025
- **Version:** 1.1 (8 April 2025)
- **Purpose:** Update the copyright and licence notice (CC BY-ND to CC BY 4.0); amend variations and additions for Western Australia; amend the variation for Tasmania regarding Part H8; correct errors.
- **Source:** [NCC 2022 Amendment 1](https://ncc.abcb.gov.au/sites/default/files/resources/2025/NCC%202022%20Amdt%201%20v1.1.pdf)[^amdt1]

# NCC 2022 Amendment 2

- **In force from:** 29 July 2025
- **Version:** 1.0 (28 July 2025)
- **Purpose:** Align the NCC with the Access Code in the Disability (Access to Premises — Buildings) Standards 2010 (AS 1428.1 updated to the 2021 edition).
- **Source:** [NCC 2022 Amendment 2](https://www.abcb.gov.au/sites/default/files/resources/2025/NCC%202022%20Amdt%202_0.pdf)[^amdt2]

# Preface-only changes

- (NCC 2022 Amendment 1) All volumes: copyright and licence notice replaced (now CC BY 4.0, excluding third-party material, trade marks, images and photographs); attribution wording replaced; Schedule 2 Table 1 Table Note (6) deleted and notes renumbered; Amendment 1 added to the history of adoption; 'List of amendments' section deleted.
- (NCC 2022 Amendment 1) WA Introduction (Volume One, Volume Two and Housing Provisions) replaced to list what the WA variations cover, with a note that the Building Regulations 2012, regulation 15C makes other modifications to the BCA.
- (NCC 2022 Amendment 2) Amendment 2 added to the history of adoption, and a List of amendments added to Volumes One and Three.

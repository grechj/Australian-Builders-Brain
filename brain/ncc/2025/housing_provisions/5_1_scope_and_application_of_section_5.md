From **NCC 2025 Housing Provisions**, Masonry.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). 

<a id="5_1_1"></a>

### 5.1.1 Scope

(1) This Section sets out the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for—

- (a) masonry veneer — see [Part 5.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_2_masonry_veneer.md); and
- (b) *cavity* masonry — see [Part 5.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_3_cavity_masonry.md); and
- (c) single leaf *unreinforced masonry* — see [Part 5.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_4_unreinforced_single_leaf_masonry.md); and
- (d) isolated masonry piers — see [Part 5.5](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_5_isolated_piers.md); and
- (e) masonry components and accessories — see [Part 5.6](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md); and
- (f) weatherproofing of masonry — see [Part 5.7](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_7_weatherproofing_of_masonry.md).

(2) For other masonry provisions not included in this Section, refer to NCC Volume Two: [H1D5(4)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5) *Reinforced masonry*.

<a id="5_1_2"></a>

### 5.1.2 Application

The application of this Section is subject to the following:

- (a) The Governing Requirements of NCC Volume Two.
- (b) Any conditions set out within the following *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of NCC Volume Two:
  - (1) [H1D5(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5), for masonry veneer.
  - (2) [H1D5(2)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5), for *cavity* masonry.
  - (3) [H1D5(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5), for *unreinforced masonry*.
  - (4) [H1D5(5)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5), for isolated masonry piers.
  - (5) [H1D5(6)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5), for masonry accessories.
  - (6) [H2D4(2)(c)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h2_damp_and_weatherproofing.md#h2d4), for weatherproofing of masonry.
- (c) The State and Territory variations, additions and deletions contained in the Schedules to the ABCB Housing Provisions and NCC Volume Two.

> **Explanatory**
>
> In NCC 2019, the content of Section 5 of the ABCB Housing Provisions (other than content added in NCC 2022 or later) was contained in the acceptable construction practices for Parts 3.3.5 and 3.3.6 of NCC 2019 Volume Two.
>
> NCC 2019 Volume Two did not include an acceptable construction practice for Parts 3.3.1, 3.3.2, 3.3.3 or 3.3.4.

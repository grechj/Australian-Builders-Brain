### 5.4.1 Application of Part 5.4

(1) [Part 5.4]() applies subject to the limitations set out in [H1D5(3)(c)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5).

(2) [Part 5.4]() need not be complied with if [H1D5(3)(a)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5) or [(b)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5) are complied with.

### 5.4.2 External walls

(1) Single leaf *unreinforced masonry* walls with *engaged piers* and return walls must comply with the relevant provisions of this Part and be constructed in accordance with the following:

- (a) The roof frame must be connected continuously to the top of the wall (see [Figure 5.4.2a](#figure_390219f9)).
- (b) *Stack bonded piers* must have wall ties at every fourth course.
- (c) Pier and return supports size limitations for—
  - (1) single leaf *unreinforced masonry* walls with *engaged piers*, must comply with [Table 5.4.2a](#table_04005049) and [Figure 5.4.2b](#figure_c5730f63); and
  - (2) single leaf *unreinforced masonry* walls with return supports, must comply with [Table 5.4.2b](#table_5576f954) and [Figure 5.4.2c](#figure_54b978e0).
- (d) An *engaged pier* or return wall must be provided at both sides of an opening.
- (e) The width of an opening must be not more than the spacing between the *engaged piers* unless the *engaged piers* either side of the opening are designed in accordance with AS 3700.
- (f) Articulation joints must be located within 300 mm of vertical supports in accordance with [5.6.8](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_8).

(2) A Class 10a building containing not more than 1 storey may be enclosed with single leaf masonry *external walls* not less than 90 mm in thickness, provided that—

- (a) the building measured in the direction of the span of the roof is not more than 9 m and the height is not more than 2.7m; and
- (b) *engaged piers* are provided that are in accordance with [Tables 5.4.2c](#table_d8be17cb) and [5.4.2d](#table_0218dda1); and
- (c) the roof does not place any spreading thrust onto the *external walls*; and
- (d) the Class 10a building is located in an area with a wind class of not more than N2.

### 5.4.3 Internal walls

Internal masonry walls must be engaged with other walls, must comply with the relevant provisions of this Part and must be—

- (a) not less than 75 mm thick; and
- (b) supported by either—
  - (1) the ceiling structure in accordance with [Figure 5.4.3a](#figure_a1bc8ceb); or
  - (2) return walls in accordance with [Figure 5.4.3b](#figure_1626a459).

<a id="figure_a1bc8ceb"></a>*Figure: Support for internal walls — supported by ceiling structure* (not reproduced here, see the figure on ncc.abcb.gov.au)

> - (1) Timber joist must be fixed to top plate in accordance with [H1D6(4)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d6).
> - (2) Fixing of top plate to masonry wall must be in accordance with [5.6.6](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_6).
>

<a id="figure_1626a459"></a>*Figure: Support for internal walls — supported by return walls* (not reproduced here, see the figure on ncc.abcb.gov.au)

> - (1) An opening of not more than 600 mm x 900 mm is allowed to internal walls without additional support.
> - (2) Openings larger than as described in Note 1 and door openings must be supported.
> - (3) The maximum allowable height for the wall is described in [Figure 5.4.3a](#figure_a1bc8ceb).
> - (4) Articulation joints must be in accordance with [5.6.8](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_8).
>

> **Explanatory**
>
> A full height door frame or stud fastened at the roof framing and tied to the wall at 300 mm centres can be considered equivalent to a return wall.
>

<a id="5_4_4"></a>

### 5.4.4 Vertical articulation joints

Vertical articulation joints must be provided in accordance with [5.6.8](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_8).

### 5.4.5 Damp-proof courses and flashing materials

Where required, *damp-proof courses* and *flashing* must be provided in accordance with [5.7.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_7_weatherproofing_of_masonry.md#5_7_3) and [5.7.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_7_weatherproofing_of_masonry.md#5_7_4).

### 9.1.1 Scope

(1) This Section sets out the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for—

- (a) fire separation of *external walls* (see [Part 9.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/9_2_fire_separation_of_external_walls.md)); and
- (b) fire protection of *separating walls* (see [Part 9.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/9_3_fire_protection_of_separating_walls_and_floors.md)); and
- (c) fire separation of garage top dwellings (see [Part 9.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/9_4_fire_protection_of_garage_top_dwellings.md)); and
- (d) smoke alarms and evacuation lighting (see [Part 9.5](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/9_5_smoke_alarms_and_evacuation_lighting.md)).

> **NSW variation**: NSW replaces subclause (1).
>

<a id="9_1_2"></a>

### 9.1.2 Application

The application of this Section is subject to the following:

- (a) The Governing Requirements of NCC Volume Two.
- (b) The State and Territory variations, additions and deletions contained in the Schedules to the ABCB Housing Provisions and NCC Volume Two.

> **Explanatory**
>
> In NCC 2019, the content of Section 9 of the ABCB Housing Provisions (other than content added in NCC 2022 or later) was contained in the acceptable construction practice for Parts 3.7.2 to 3.7.5 of NCC 2019 Volume Two.
>
> The content of Part 3.7.1 has been retained within [Part H3](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h3_fire_safety.md) of NCC Volume Two as it contains requirements which affect how other provisions referenced in [Part H3](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h3_fire_safety.md) are applied.

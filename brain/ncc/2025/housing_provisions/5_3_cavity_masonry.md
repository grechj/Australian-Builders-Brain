From **NCC 2025 Housing Provisions**, Masonry.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). 

<a id="5_3_1"></a>

### 5.3.1 Application

(1) [Part 5.3]() applies subject to the limitations set out in [H1D5(2)(c)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5).

(2) [Part 5.3]() need not be complied with if [H1D5(2)(a)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5) or [(b)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5) are complied with.

<a id="5_3_2"></a>

### 5.3.2 Height of wall limitation

*Cavity* masonry walls must not be greater than 8.5 m in height when measured above the adjacent *finished ground level*.

<a id="5_3_3"></a>

### 5.3.3 External walls

(1) *Cavity* masonry walls must comply with the relevant provisions of this Part and [Parts 5.6](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md) and [5.7](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_7_weatherproofing_of_masonry.md), and be constructed as follows:

- (a) The height of the wall between *lateral supports* (floor or ceiling or roof diaphragm) must be not more than 3 m.
- (b) *Cavity* masonry walls subject to wind loads must be supported by masonry cross walls or by steel mullions complying with (3).
- (c) Masonry cross walls must be—
  - (1) not less than 2 m in length; and
  - (2) at not more than 5.1 m centres where the length of the *cavity* wall being supported does not contain any opening or control joint; and
  - (3) not more than 2.5 m from the edge of a control joint in the length of the *cavity* wall being supported; and
  - (4) not more than a distance from the edge of an opening in the length of the *cavity* wall being supported as stated in [Table 5.3.3](#table_a608e57d); and
  - (5) located at both edges of openings of width greater than 2.7 m; and
  - (6) directly connected to the internal leaf of the *cavity* wall being supported using—
    - (1) properly bonded units with at least 90 mm engagement on each side of the interface with the selected bond pattern but not less than every fourth course of masonry; or
    - (2) medium duty Type A *cavity* wall ties in aligning mortar bed joints at a vertical spacing of not more than 300 mm; and
  - (7) connected by a floor or ceiling diaphragm to the wall being supported where floor or ceiling connections are designed in accordance with AS/NZS 4600, AS 1720.1 or AS 3600, as appropriate.

(2) *Cavity* masonry walls must be constructed of two leaves, with each leaf not less than 90 mm wide.

(3) In *cavity* masonry construction, a *cavity* must be provided between the inner and outer masonry leaves as follows:

- (a) The *cavity* must be not less than 35 mm and not more than 75 mm in width, in accordance with [5.7.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_7_weatherproofing_of_masonry.md#5_7_2).
- (b) Except for steel mullions, the minimum *cavity* width specified in (a) is to be maintained between the outer masonry leaf and any insulation or services located in the *cavity*.
- (c) Where steel mullions are located in a *cavity* as permitted by (b), a vertical *damp-proof course* must be placed between the outer masonry leaf and the mullion to prevent moisture penetration.

<a id="table_a608e57d"></a>**Table 5.3.3: Spacing of return walls for cavity walls with openings — distance from the edge of an opening (mm)**

| Wind class | Opening width (mm) 900 | 1500 | 2100 | 2700 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N3 | 2100 | 1800 | 800 | 400 |
| N2 | 3200 | 2900 | 2600 | 2300 |
| N1 | 2500<sup>(Note)</sup> | 2200<sup>(Note)</sup> | 1900<sup>(Note)</sup> | 800<sup>(Note)</sup> |

> The spacing in wind class N1 is smaller than for N2 because [5.6.5](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_5) states that for *cavity* walls in wind class N1, light duty *cavity* ties are to be used. This results in only relying on one leaf to resist the load instead of sharing it equally as per clause 7.7.3 of AS 3700.
>

> **Explanatory**
>
> Steel mullions complying with AS 4773.1 and 4773.2 used to support wind loads may be placed within a *cavity*. Flat ceiling capable of performing diaphragm action may act as *lateral support* to walls provided the structure has been specifically designed.
>

<a id="5_3_4"></a>

### 5.3.4 Internal walls

(1) Where internal masonry walls intersect with other internal or external walls they must comply with the relevant provisions of this Part and be—

- (a) not less than 75 mm thick; and
- (b) either—
  - (1) bonded at the junctions of the intersecting walls; or
  - (2) provided with an articulation joint in accordance with [5.6.8](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_8).

(2) Where a vertical articulation joint is provided in an internal masonry wall it must be formed in accordance with [5.6.8](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_8).

<a id="5_3_5"></a>

### 5.3.5 Openings in cavity masonry

(1) Except where excluded by (2), openings in *cavity* masonry must be spanned by steel lintels in accordance with [5.6.7](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_7).

(2) Openings in *cavity* masonry not more than 500 mm wide need not be provided with a steel lintel provided the opening is adequately supported.

> **Explanatory**
>
> An opening of not more than 500 mm is considered to be adequately supported if the masonry bears directly on a timber window head or steel frame.
>

<a id="5_3_6"></a>

### 5.3.6 Damp-proof courses and flashing materials

*Damp-proof courses* and *flashing* must be provided in accordance with [5.7.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_7_weatherproofing_of_masonry.md#5_7_3) and [5.7.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_7_weatherproofing_of_masonry.md#5_7_4).

<a id="5_3_7"></a>

### 5.3.7 Vertical articulation joints

Vertical articulation joints are to be installed in accordance with [5.6.8](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/5_6_masonry_components_and_accessories.md#5_6_8).

### 3.2.1 Un-retained bulk earthworks – site cut and fill

(1) A *site* cut using an un-retained embankment must be—

- (a) within the *allotment*; and
- (b) not within the zone of influence of any existing structure on the property, or the *allotment* boundary as defined in [Table 3.2.1](#table_a91b3ac6) and [Figure 3.2.1a](#figure_bc073cf2); and
- (c) not deeper than 2 m from the natural ground level at any point.

(2) Fill, using an un-retained embankment must—

- (a) be placed within the *allotment*; and
- (b) be placed at a gradient which complies with [Table 3.2.1](#table_a91b3ac6) and [Figure 3.2.1b](#figure_37666e23); and
- (c) be placed and mechanically compacted in layers not more than 150 mm; and
- (d) be not more than 2 m in height from the natural ground level at any point; and
- (e) where used to support footings or slabs, be placed and compacted in accordance with [Part 4.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/4_2_footings_slabs_and_associated_elements.md); and
- (f) have *surface water* diverted away from any existing structure on the property or adjoining *allotment* in accordance with [3.3.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/3_3_drainage.md#3_3_3).

<a id="table_a91b3ac6"></a>**Table 3.2.1: Un-retained embankment slope ratios**

| Soil class (see [4.2.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/4_2_footings_slabs_and_associated_elements.md#table_d9bea1e4) for material description) | Site cut (excavation) (maximum embankment slope ratio, angle of site cut H:L <sup>Note 1</sup>) | Compacted fill (maximum embankment slope ratio, angle of batter H:L <sup>Note 1</sup>) |
|---|---|---|
| Stable rock (Class A) | 8:1 | 2:3 |
| Sand (Class A) | 1:2 | 1:2 |
| Firm clay (Class M-E) | 1:1 | 1:2 |
| Soft clay (Class M-E) | 2:3 | Not suitable |

> - (1) See [Figures 3.2.1a](#figure_bc073cf2) and [3.2.1b](#figure_37666e23) for some examples of un-retained embankment slopes.
> - (2) Retaining walls must be installed in accordance with [H1D3(2)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d3) where—
>   - (1) the embankment slope is steeper than described in this Table; or
>   - (2) the soil type is not described in this Table.

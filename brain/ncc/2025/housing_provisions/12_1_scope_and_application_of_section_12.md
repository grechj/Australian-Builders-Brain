### 12.1.1 Scope

(1) This Section sets out the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for—

- (a) construction in *alpine areas* (see [Part 12.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/12_2_construction_in_alpine_areas.md)); and
- (b) attachment of decks and balconies to external walls (see [Part 12.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/12_3_attachment_of_framed_decks_and_balconies_to_external_walls_of_buildings_using_a_waling_plate.md)); and
- (c) heating appliances, fire places, chimneys and flues (see [Part 12.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/12_4_heating_appliances_fireplaces_chimneys_and_flues.md)).

(2) For other ancillary provisions and additional construction requirements not included in this Section of the ABCB Housing Provisions, refer to the following *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* in NCC Volume Two:

- (a) *swimming pools* (see [H7D2](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h7_ancillary_provisions_and_additional_construction_requirements.md#h7d2)).
- (b) earthquake areas (see [H1D9](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d9)).
- (c) *flood hazard areas* (see [H1D10](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d10)).
- (d) construction in *designated bushfire prone areas* (see [H7D4](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h7_ancillary_provisions_and_additional_construction_requirements.md#h7d4)).

<a id="12_1_2"></a>

### 12.1.2 Application

The application of this Section is subject to the following:

- (a) The Governing Requirements of NCC Volume Two.
- (b) The State and Territory variations, additions and deletions contained in the Schedules to the ABCB Housing Provisions and NCC Volume Two.

> **Explanatory**
>
> In NCC 2019, the content of Section 12 of the ABCB Housing Provisions (other than content added in NCC 2022 or later) was contained in the acceptable construction practices for Parts 3.10.4, 3.10.6 and 3.10.7 of NCC 2019 Volume Two.
>
> In NCC 2019 Volume Two, Parts 3.10.1, 3.10.2, 3.10.3 and 3.10.5 did not include an acceptable construction practice.
>

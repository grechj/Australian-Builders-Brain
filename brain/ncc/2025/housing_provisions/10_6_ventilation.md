### 10.6.1 Application

(1) [Part 10.6]() applies subject to the limitations set out at [H4D7](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h4_health_and_amenity.md#h4d7).

(2) [Part 10.6]() need not be complied with if [H4D7(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h4_health_and_amenity.md#h4d7) is complied with.

> **Explanatory**
>
> The requirements of this Part are to be read in conjunction with the condensation management requirements in [Part 10.8](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/10_8_condensation_management.md) and the ceiling fan requirements in [Part 13.5](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_5_ceiling_fans.md). However, it should be noted that [Part 13.5](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_5_ceiling_fans.md) does not apply in all States and Territories.

### 10.6.2 Ventilation requirements

Ventilation must be provided to a *habitable room*, *sanitary compartment*, bathroom, shower room, laundry and any other room occupied by a person for any purpose by any of the following means:

- (a) Openings, *windows*, doors or other devices which can be opened—
  - (1) with a ventilating area not less than 5% of the *floor area* of the room *required* to be ventilated; and
  - (2) open to—
    - (1) a suitably sized court, or space open to the sky; or
    - (2) an open verandah, carport, or the like; or
    - (3) an adjoining room in accordance with (b).
- (b) Natural ventilation to a room may come through a *window*, opening, door or other device from an adjoining room (including an enclosed verandah) if—
  - (1) the room to be ventilated or the adjoining room is not a *sanitary compartment*; and
  - (2) the *window*, opening, door or other device has a ventilating area of not less than 5% of the *floor area* of the room to be ventilated; and
  - (3) the adjoining room has a *window*, opening, door or other device with a ventilating area of not less than 5% of the combined *floor areas* of both rooms; and
  - (4) the ventilating areas specified may be reduced as appropriate if direct natural ventilation is provided from another source (See [Figure 10.6.2](#figure_9c23ee8f)).
- (c) An exhaust fan or other means of mechanical ventilation may be used to ventilate a *sanitary compartment*, laundry, kitchen or bathroom, or where mechanical ventilation is provided in accordance with [10.6.3(b)](#10_6_3), provided contaminated air exhausts comply with [10.8.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/10_8_condensation_management.md#10_8_2).

> **Exemption**
>
> 10.6.2 does not apply to rooms or spaces of a specialised nature occupied neither frequently nor for extended periods.
>

> **Explanatory**
>
> The ventilating area of a *window* is measured as the size of the openable sash of the *window*. This is the case regardless of the type of *window*, i.e. whether it is an awning, casement or sliding *window* and irrespective of the restrictions on the openable sash.
>
> 10.6.2(b) permits a room’s *required* ventilation to be ‘borrowed’ from an adjoining room, i.e. an adjoining room’s ventilation can be used to help make up the total amount of ventilation *required*.
>
> The use of borrowed ventilation is acceptable if the provisions of 10.6.2(b) are applied to the subject room and to the total area of each relevant room.
>

> **Explanatory: Exemptions**
>
> The exemptions provided for 10.6.2 remove the need for rooms and spaces, such as pantries, walk-in-robes, storage rooms, corridors and the like, to be provided with ventilation.
>

### 10.6.3 Location of sanitary compartments

A *sanitary compartment* must not open directly into a kitchen or pantry unless—

- (a) access is by an airlock, hallway or other room, (see [Figure 10.6.3](#figure_02217fba)); or
- (b) the *sanitary compartment* is provided with an exhaust fan or other means of mechanical exhaust ventilation.

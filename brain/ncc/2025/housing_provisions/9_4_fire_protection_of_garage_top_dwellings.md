## Part 9.4 Fire protection of garage top dwellings

> **NSW variation**: NSW adds 9.4.1 Fire separation.
>
> (1) A *garage top dwelling* must be separated from a non-associated *private garage* by a floor complying with [NSW 9.4.2](#9_4_2).
>
> (2) Where a *garage top dwelling* is served by an internal stair, the *garage top dwelling* must be separated from a non-associated *private garage* by a wall complying with [NSW 9.4.3](#9_4_3).
>
> (3) Where a *garage top dwelling* is located above both associated and non-associated *private garages* —
>
> - (a) in addition to a floor *required* by (1), the *private garages* must be separated with a wall complying with [NSW 9.4.3](#9_4_3); or
> - (b) where a *garage top dwelling* is not served by an internal stair, the *garage top dwelling* may be separated from the *private garages* by a floor complying with [NSW 9.4.2](#9_4_2).
>

> **NSW variation**: NSW adds 9.4.2 Construction of floors.
>
> (1) A floor *required* by [NSW 9.4.1(1)](#9_4_1) or [NSW 9.4.1(3)(b)](#9_4_1) must—
>
> - (a) have an FRL of not less than 30/30/30 when tested from the underside; or
> - (b) have a *fire-protective covering* on the underside of the floor, including beams incorporated in it; or
> - (c) be a floor/ceiling system incorporating a ceiling which has a *resistance to the incipient spread of fire* to the space above itself of not less than 60 minutes.
>
> (2) Where a floor subject to (1)(a) depends on direct vertical or lateral support from another part to maintain its FRL, that supporting part must have an FRL of not less than 30/-/-.
>
> (3) Where a service passes through a floor referred to in (1), the penetration must not reduce the performance of the floor or covering.
>

> **NSW variation**: NSW adds 9.4.3 Construction of walls.
>
> (1) A wall *required* by [NSW 9.4.1(2)](#9_4_1) or [NSW 9.4.1(3)(a)](#9_4_1) must—
>
> - (a) have an FRL of not less than 30/30/30 when tested from the non-associated *private garage* side; or
> - (b) be of masonry construction not less than 90 mm thick.
>
> (2) A wall subject to (1) must—
>
> - (a) commence at the footings or ground slab; and
> - (b) extend to the underside of a floor complying with [NSW 9.4.2](#9_4_2).
>
> (3) A wall referred to in (1)(a), if of *lightweight construction* must be tested in accordance with [Specification 6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_6_structural_tests_for_lightweight_construction.md) of Volume One.
>
> (4) Where a service passes through a wall referred to in (1), the penetration must not reduce the performance of the wall.
>
> (5) A wall *required* by [NSW 9.4.1(2)](#9_4_1) or [NSW 9.4.1(3)(a)](#9_4_1) need not comply with [9.3.1](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/9_3_fire_protection_of_separating_walls_and_floors.md#9_3_1).
>

### 9.4.1 Walls requiring protection

(1) Where parts of a Class 1a dwelling are located above a Class 10a *private garage* that is not associated with the Class 1a dwelling—

- (a) any wall separating parts of the Class 1a dwelling from the *private garage* not associated with the dwelling must comply with (2); and
- (b) any *private garage* associated with and located below the Class 1a dwelling must be separated from the *private garage* not associated with the dwelling by a wall complying with (2).

(2) A wall *required* by (1) must—

- (a) have either—
  - (1) an FRL of not less than 60/60/60 when tested from the *private garage* associated with another dwelling side; or
  - (2) be of masonry construction not less than 90 mm thick; and
- (b) commence at the footings or ground slab; and
- (c) extend to the underside of a separating floor complying with [9.4.2](#9_4_2); and
- (d) comply with [9.3.1(2)](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/9_3_fire_protection_of_separating_walls_and_floors.md#9_3_1) to [(5)](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/9_3_fire_protection_of_separating_walls_and_floors.md#9_3_1) and [9.3.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/9_3_fire_protection_of_separating_walls_and_floors.md#9_3_2) as applicable.

<a id="9_4_2"></a>

### 9.4.2 Separating floors

(1) Where parts of a Class 1a dwelling are located above or below a Class 10a *private garage* that is not associated with the Class 1a dwelling, any floor separating the Class 1a dwelling from the Class 10a *private garage* not associated with the dwelling must—

- (a) be a floor/ceiling or floor/soffit system incorporating a ceiling or soffit which has a *resistance to the incipient spread of fire* to the space above itself of not less than 60 minutes; or
- (b) have an FRL not less than 30/30/30 when tested from the underside; or
- (c) have a *fire-protective covering* on the underside of the floor, including beams incorporated in it, if the floor is *combustible* or of metal.

(2) Where a floor subject to (1)(b) depends on direct vertical or lateral support from another part to maintain its FRL, that supporting part must have an FRL of not less than 30/-/-.

(3) Where a service passes through a floor referred to in (1), the penetration must not reduce the fire performance of the floor or covering.

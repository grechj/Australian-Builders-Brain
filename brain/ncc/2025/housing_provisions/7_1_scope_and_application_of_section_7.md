From **NCC 2025 Housing Provisions**, Roof and wall cladding.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). 

<a id="7_1_1"></a>

### 7.1.1 Scope

(1) This Section sets out the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for—

- (a) metal sheet roofing (see [Part 7.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/7_2_sheet_roofing.md)); and
- (b) roof tiles (see [Part 7.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/7_3_roof_tiles_and_shingles.md)); and
- (c) gutters and downpipes (see [Part 7.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/7_4_gutters_and_downpipes.md)); and
- (d) timber and composite wall cladding (see [Part 7.5](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/7_5_timber_and_composite_wall_cladding.md)).

(2) For other roof and wall cladding provisions not included in this Section, refer to NCC Volume Two [H1D7(5)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d7) Metal wall cladding.

> **Explanatory**
>
> This Part contains requirements including weatherproofing and structural requirements, for wall and roof systems. Gutter and downpipe requirements are also contained in this Part.
>
> It should be noted that other construction methods may be used to achieve the same results as specified in this Part provided they comply with the appropriate *Performance Requirements*.
>

<a id="7_1_2"></a>

### 7.1.2 Application

The application of this Section is subject to the following:

- (a) The Governing Requirements of NCC Volume Two.
- (b) The State and Territory variations, additions and deletions contained in the Schedules to the ABCB Housing Provisions and NCC Volume Two.

> **Explanatory**
>
> In NCC 2019, the content of Section 7 of the ABCB Housing Provisions (other than content added in NCC 2022 or later) was contained in the acceptable construction practices for Parts 3.5.1 to 3.5.4 of NCC 2019 Volume Two.
>
> NCC 2019 Volume Two did not include an acceptable construction practice for Part 3.5.5.
>

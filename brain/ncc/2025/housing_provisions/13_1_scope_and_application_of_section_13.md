## NSW variation: NSW deletes Part 13.1

This Part has been deliberately left blank. Section 13.1 of the ABCB Housing Provisions 2025 does not apply in NSW and Section 13.1 of ABCB Housing Provisions 2022 Amendment 2 applies in NSW.

## Part 13.1 Scope and application of Section 13

> **NT variation**: NT adds 13.1.1 Scope.
>
> This Section sets out the following *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for energy efficiency:
>
> - (a) Building fabric (see [NT Part 13.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_2_building_fabric.md#part_ariation)).
> - (b) External glazing (see [NT Part 13.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_3_external_glazing.md#part_ariation)).
> - (c) Building sealing (see [NT Part 13.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_4_building_sealing.md#part_ariation)).
> - (d) Air movement (see [NT Part 13.5](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_5_ceiling_fans.md#part_ariation)).
> - (e) Services (see [NT Part 13.7](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_7_services.md#part_ariation)).
>

> **NT variation**: NT adds 13.1.2 Application.
>
> The application of this Section is subject to the following:
>
> - (a) The Governing Requirements of NCC Volume Two.
> - (b) The Northern Territory variations, additions and deletions contained in the Schedule 6 to NCC Volume Two.
>

## TAS variation: TAS deletes Part 13.1

### 13.1.1 Scope

This Section sets out the following *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for energy efficiency:

- (a) Building fabric (see [Part 13.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_2_building_fabric.md)).
- (b) External glazing (see [Part 13.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_3_external_glazing.md)).
- (c) Building sealing (see [Part 13.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_4_building_sealing.md)).
- (d) Ceiling fans (see [Part 13.5](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_5_ceiling_fans.md)).
- (e) Whole-of-home energy usage (see [Part 13.6](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_6_whole_of_home_energy_usage.md)).
- (f) Services (see [Part 13.7](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_7_services.md)).

### 5.7.1 Application

(1) [Part 5.7]() applies subject to the limitation set out in [H2D4(2)(c)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h2_damp_and_weatherproofing.md#h2d4).

(2) [Part 5.7]() need not be complied with if [H2D4(2)(a)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h2_damp_and_weatherproofing.md#h2d4) or [(b)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h2_damp_and_weatherproofing.md#h2d4) are complied with.

<a id="5_7_2"></a>

### 5.7.2 Cavities

(1) For masonry veneer, the clear width of a *cavity* between the masonry veneer and the exterior face of the supporting frame must not be—

- (a) less than 25 mm wide; and
- (b) more than 75 mm wide.

(2) For *cavity* masonry, the clear width of a *cavity* between the inner and outer masonry leaves must not be—

- (a) less than 35 mm; and
- (b) more than 75 mm.

(3) Where masonry veneer and *cavity* masonry in (1) and (2) are constructed on a slab-on-ground, the *cavity* must be drained to the outside in accordance with [5.7.5](#5_7_5).

(4) The exterior masonry leaf must not overhang the edge of the slab by more than 15 mm.

> **Explanatory**
>
> The 25 mm clear width of the *cavity* needs to be maintained regardless of any wall *membranes*, sheet bracing or services installed to the supporting frame.
>
> Where mullions are located within a *cavity*, a vertical *damp-proof course* must be placed between the outer masonry leaf and the mullion to prevent moisture penetration.
>

<a id="5_7_3"></a>

### 5.7.3 Damp-proof courses and flashings – material

*Damp-proof courses* and *flashings* must consist of—

- (a) a material that complies with AS/NZS 2904; or
- (b) embossed black polyethylene film of high impact resistance and low slip, with a nominal thickness of 0.5 mm prior to embossing, and comply with clause 7.6 of AS/NZS 2904; or
- (c) polyethylene coated metal, that has an aluminium core of not less than 0.1 mm thick, is coated both sides with bitumen adhesive enclosed in polyethylene film of not less than 0.1 mm thick on each face, and has a nominal total thickness of not less than 0.5 mm prior to embossing; or
- (d) bitumen impregnated materials of not less than 2.5 mm thick, that comply with clause 7.5 of AS/NZS 2904; or
- (e) termite sheet materials complying with [Part 3.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/3_4_termite_risk_management.md) (with no penetrations) serving the purpose of a *damp-proof course* and/or *flashing* that is continuous through the wall or pier.

<a id="5_7_4"></a>

### 5.7.4 Damp-proof courses and flashings – installation

(1) *Damp-proof courses* and *flashings* must be—

- (a) located so as to form a continuous damp-proofing barrier—
  - (1) around the bottom perimeter of walls where constructed on a concrete slab; and
  - (2) in walls and piers below suspended floors; and
  - (3) where a masonry wall passes through a roof; and
  - (4) where a roof abuts an external masonry wall; and
  - (5) to the bottom and tops of *windows* and doors and the like in accordance with (3), except a *damp-proof course* or a *flashing* need not be provided to the top of a *window* or door where the opening is protected by an eave of a width more than 3 times the height of the masonry veneer above the opening; and
- (b) continuous through the wall or pier and be visible from the outside face of the wall.

(2) The location of a *damp-proof course*, or *flashing* serving as a *damp-proof course*, must be not less than—

- (a) 150 mm above the adjacent ground level; or
- (b) 75 mm above the finished surface level of adjacent paved, concreted or landscaped areas that slope away from the wall; or
- (c) 50 mm above finished paved, concreted or landscaped areas complying with [3.3.3(b)(ii)](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/3_3_drainage.md#3_3_3) and protected from the direct effects of the weather by a carport, verandah or the like; or
- (d) in *low rainfall intensity areas* —
  - (1) 15 mm above finished paved, concreted or landscaped areas; or
  - (2) 0 mm above finished paved, concreted or landscaped areas if the *damp-proof course* is protected from the direct effects of the weather by a carport, verandah or the like.

(3) Sill and head *flashings* serving openings must be—

- (a) installed so that the *flashing* extends not less than 150 mm beyond the reveals on each side of the opening; and
- (b) located not more than—
  - (1) one course below the sill brick course; and
  - (2) 300 mm above the opening; and
- (c) turned up in the *cavity* not less than 150 mm above the opening; and
- (d) embedded not less than 30 mm into—
  - (1) for masonry veneer, the masonry leaf; and
  - (2) for *cavity* masonry, the outer masonry leaf; and
- (e) attached to the *window* or wall framing.

<a id="5_7_5"></a>

### 5.7.5 Weepholes

(1) Except where excluded by (2), open perpend joints (weepholes) must be created in the course immediately above any *flashing* (including above any *damp-proof course* acting as a *flashing*) and be—

- (a) a minimum of 50 mm in height, by the width of the vertical mortar joint; and
- (b) at not more than 1.2 m centres; and

(2) Weepholes are not *required* in the following locations:

- (a) Where head openings are less than 1.2 m wide.
- (b) Beneath *window* and door sills.
- (c) Where the level of the external impervious surface is elevated for the purpose of providing step-free access *required* by [H8P1](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h8_livable_housing_design.md#h8p1).

<a id="5_7_6"></a>

### 5.7.6 Weatherproofing for single leaf masonry walls

(1) A *waterproof* coating material must be applied to all external single skin masonry walls in accordance with the following:

- (a) The coating must extend from the uppermost exposed part of the wall—
  - (1) to a level adjacent to the internal finished floor level, if the external masonry wall leaf overhangs the edge of the slab by not less than 10 mm; or
  - (2) 50 mm below the internal floor level if no edge overhang is provided.
- (b) Acceptable external *waterproof* finishes are—
  - (1) three coats of 100% acrylic based exterior quality gloss paint; or
  - (2) one complete coat of cement based paint and two coats of 100% acrylic based exterior quality gloss paint; or
  - (3) clear water repellent, provided the wall is protected by a roof overhang of not less than 1500 mm.

(2) *Windows* must be installed in accordance with [Figure 5.7.6](#figure_2670895c).

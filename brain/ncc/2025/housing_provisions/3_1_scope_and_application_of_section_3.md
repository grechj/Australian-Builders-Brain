### 3.1.1 Scope

(1) This Section sets out the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for—

- (a) earthworks — [Part 3.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/3_2_earthworks.md); and
- (b) drainage — [Part 3.3](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/3_3_drainage.md); and
- (c) termite risk management — [Part 3.4](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/3_4_termite_risk_management.md).

(2) For other site preparation provisions not included in this Section, refer to NCC Volume Two: [H1D3(2)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d3) Earth retaining structures.

> **Explanatory**
>
> These provisions relate to general *site* preparation for footings, services, drainage and installation of termite management systems. It should be noted that other construction methods may be used to achieve the same results as specified in this Part provided they comply with the appropriate *Performance Requirement*.
>
> The provisions in [Part 3.2](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/3_2_earthworks.md) will enable earthworks associated with the construction of a building to be carried out safely and to avoid potential damage to the subject building, adjoining structures and property through the soil collapsing or subsiding. Exceptional *site* conditions (including the effects of torrential rain) may need special consideration and additional advice from appropriately qualified people should be considered.
>
> State and Territory legislation may also have requirements that apply to earthworks, especially in relation to adjoining property and notification of owners of that property. Advice should be obtained from the *appropriate authority* before commencement of works.
>
> The requirements of this Part are to be read in conjunction with [H1D3(2)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d3) of NCC Volume Two where an earth retaining structure is installed.
>

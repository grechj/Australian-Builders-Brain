### 12.4.1 Application

For the installation of a domestic solid fuel burning appliance, [Part 12.4]() need not be complied with if [H7D5(a)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h7_ancillary_provisions_and_additional_construction_requirements.md#h7d5) is complied with.

<a id="12_4_2"></a>

### 12.4.2 Open fireplace construction

An open fireplace, or solid-fuel burning appliance in which the fuel-burning compartment is not enclosed must have—

- (a) all masonry constructed in accordance with [H1D5](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5); and
- (b) a hearth constructed of stone, concrete, masonry or similar *non-combustible* material so that—
  - (1) it extends not less than 300 mm beyond the front of the fireplace opening and not less than 150 mm beyond each side of that opening; and
  - (2) its upper surface does not slope away from the back hearth (see [Figure 12.4.2](#figure_49776688)); and
  - (3) *combustible* material, such as flooring or framing members below or around the external edge of the hearth, is situated not less than 150 mm from the upper surface of the hearth (see [Figure 12.4.2](#figure_49776688)); and
- (c) walls forming the sides and back of the fireplace up to a height of 300 mm above the underside of the arch or lintel which—
  - (1) are constructed in 2 separate leaves of solid masonry with a total combined thickness not less than 180 mm, excluding any *cavity*; and
  - (2) do not consist of concrete block masonry in the construction of the inner leaf; and
  - (3) are constructed of masonry units with a net volume, excluding cored and similar holes, not less than 75% of their gross volume, measured on the overall rectangular shape of the units, and with an actual thickness of not less than 100 mm; and
- (d) the fireplace must be constructed on footings complying with [4.2.18](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/4_2_footings_slabs_and_associated_elements.md#4_2_18).

### 12.4.3 Chimney construction

The construction of a chimney must comply with [H1D5](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5) and the following:

- (a) The walls of the chimney above the level referred to in [12.4.2(c)](#12_4_2) must be lined internally to a thickness of not less than 10 mm with composition mortar parging.
- (b) The composition mortar in (a) must comply with AS 3700 or AS 4773 except that the mortar must be mixed by volume in the proportions of 1 part cement : 1 part lime : 5 parts sand.
- (c) The chimney or flue must terminate not less than 300 mm above the highest part of the building within a horizontal distance of 3.6 m of the chimney or flue (see [Figure 12.4.3](#figure_3d82271c)).

### 12.4.4 Installation of insert fireplaces and flues

An insert fireplace and flue must comply with the following:

- (a) The insert fireplace and flue must be—
  - (1) tested and passed the tests required by AS/NZS 2918; and
  - (2) fitted into a masonry fireplace (including chimney) constructed in accordance with [H1D5](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5) and [Figure 12.4.4](#figure_10a02a8d).
- (b) The flue must be double skin and have been tested and pass the tests required by AS/NZS 2918.
- (c) There must be a clearance of 50 mm between the outer flue and adjacent materials.
- (d) The flue must terminate in accordance with [Figure 12.4.3](#figure_3d82271c).
- (e) The hearth must be constructed in accordance with [12.4.2(b)](#12_4_2) and [(d)](#12_4_2).

### 12.4.5 Installation of free standing heating appliances

The installation of a free standing heating appliance must comply with the following:

- (a) The appliance must—
  - (1) be installed with safety clearances determined by testing in accordance with AS/NZS 2918; or
  - (2) be located not less than 1.2 m from adjoining walls (other than a masonry wall); or
  - (3) have a heat shield between the adjoining wall (other than a masonry wall) and the heating appliance in accordance with [Figure 12.4.5a](#figure_b105907a) and [Figure 12.4.5b](#figure_6a4bcb4f).
- (b) Where a heat shield is used, it must be installed in accordance with [Figure 12.4.5a](#figure_b105907a) and [Figure 12.4.5b](#figure_6a4bcb4f), and—
  - (1) have an FRL of not less than 60/60/60; or
  - (2) be not less than 90 mm thick masonry constructed in accordance with [H1D5](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d5).
- (c) The heating appliance must be installed on a hearth—
  - (1) complying with [12.4.2(b)](#12_4_2), except that the hearth must extend 400 mm from the front and sides of the appliance in accordance with [Figure 12.4.5a](#figure_b105907a) and [Figure 12.4.5b](#figure_6a4bcb4f); or
  - (2) where a heat shield is installed, in accordance with [Figure 12.4.5a](#figure_b105907a) and [Figure 12.4.5b](#figure_6a4bcb4f).
- (d) The flue must—
  - (1) have been tested and passed the tests required by AS/NZS 2918; and
  - (2) be installed in accordance with [Figure 12.4.5c](#figure_97964767); and
  - (3) terminate in accordance with [Figure 12.4.3](#figure_3d82271c); and
  - (4) be flashed in accordance with [H1D7](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h1_structure.md#h1d7).
- (e) Flue types or installation of flues in areas not specifically covered by [Figure 12.4.5a](#figure_b105907a) and [Figure 12.4.5b](#figure_6a4bcb4f) and [Figure 12.4.5c](#figure_97964767) must be installed in accordance with AS/NZS 2918.

Where 12.4.5(a)(i) is chosen as a solution the free standing heating appliance must be installed with safety clearances determined by testing in accordance with AS/NZS 2918. 12.4.5(d)(i), in addition to (d)(ii) and (d)(iii), require the flue to be tested and have passed the tests required by AS/NZS 2918.

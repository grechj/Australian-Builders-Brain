## Part 13.5 Air movement

> **NT variation**: NT adds 13.5.1 Application of Part 13.5.
>
> This Part applies to a *habitable room* in a Class 1 building.
>

> **NT variation**: NT adds 13.5.2 Air movement.
>
> (1) Air movement must be provided to *habitable rooms* in accordance with [Table NT 13.5.2](#table_38f75470).
>
> (2) Air movement *required* by (1) may be provided through an opening from an adjoining room (including an enclosed verandah) if—
>
> - (a) the adjoining room is not a *sanitary compartment*; and
> - (b) the opening between the adjoining room and the *habitable room* complies with [Table NT 13.5.2](#table_38f75470) as if it were a *ventilation opening* to the *habitable room* or a proportion there of if some ventilation is provided from another source; and
> - (c) the *ventilation opening* to the adjoining room complies with [Table NT 13.5.2](#table_38f75470) for the total area of the floor of the adjoining room and the proportion of the *habitable room* that is ventilated from the adjoining room.
>
> <a id="table_38f75470"></a>**Table 13.5.2: Minimum total ventilation opening area as a percentage of the floor area for each habitable room**
>
> | *Climate zone* | Minimum total *ventilation opening* area per *habitable room* (percentage of the area of the floor of the *habitable room*) Without a ceiling fan or evaporative cooler | With a ceiling fan | With an evaporative cooler |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | 15% | 12.5% | 15% |
> | 3 | 12.5% | 7.5% | 7.5% |
>

> **NT variation**: NT adds 13.5.3 Ventilation openings.
>
> (1) The total *ventilation opening* area *required* by [Table NT 13.5.2](#table_38f75470) to a *habitable room* must—
>
> - (a) be connected by a breeze path complying with (b) to another *ventilation opening* in another room or space; or
> - (b) be provided by a minimum of two ventilation openings located within the same *habitable room*, with each *ventilation opening* having an area of not less than 25% of the area *required* by [Table NT 13.5.2](#table_38f75470).
>
> (2) A breeze path *required* by (1)(a) must—
>
> - (a) pass through not more than two openings in the internal walls with each opening having an area of not less than 1.5 m<sup>2</sup>; and
> - (b) have a distance along the breeze path between *ventilation opening* of not more than 20 m.
>
> > **Explanatory**
> >
> > - *Ventilation openings* should be designed to allow the interior of the building to take full advantage of any natural breeze. Careful consideration should be given to the type and location of openings to ensure optimum effect is achieved and that internal “dead air pockets” are avoided.
> > - An opening may serve more than one breeze path.
> > - Two openings are stated in NT 13.5.3(2)(a) as the limit of the number of openings permitted in a breeze path. These are typically doorways. Larger openings, such as those between adjoining lounge and dining areas in the same space are unlikely to restrict air movement significantly.
> >
>

### 13.5.2 Ceiling fans

Ceiling fans must be installed in accordance with [Table 13.5.2](#table_0f09cefd) in—

- (a) *climate zones* 1, 2 and 3; and
- (b) *climate zone* 5 in New South Wales and Queensland.

<a id="table_0f09cefd"></a>**Table 13.5.2: Minimum ceiling fan requirements in climate zones 1, 2, 3 and 5**

| Size of room (m²) | Minimum number and diameter (mm) of ceiling fans *required* in a bedroom in *climate zones* 1, 2 and 3 | Minimum number and diameter (mm) of ceiling fans *required* in a *habitable room* other than a bedroom in *climate zones* 1, 2, 3 and 5 (NSW and Qld) |
|---|---|---|
| <15 | 1 x 900 | 1 x 900 |
| ≥15<20 | 1 x 1200 | 1 x 1200 |
| ≥20<25 | 1 x 1200 | 1 x 1400 |
| ≥25<30 | 1 x 1400 | 2 x 1200 |
| ≥30<45 | 1 x 1400 | 2 x 1400 |
| ≥45<50 | 2 x 1400 | 3 x 1200 |
| ≥50 | 2 x 1400 | 3 x 1400 |

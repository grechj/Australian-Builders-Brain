From **NCC 2025 Volume Two**, Class 1 and 10 buildings.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (WA) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This part sets out the changes for the application of AS/NZS 3500.3:2021 in Western Australia.

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H10D1 Eaves gutter overflow.
>
> Delete clause 3.5.1 of AS/NZS 3500.3:2021 and replace with the following:
>
> **3.5.1 General**
>
> Eaves gutter systems, including downpipes, shall be designed and installed so that water will not flow back into the building. Overflow measures may be provided in accordance with Part 7.4 of the ABCB Housing Provisions Standard.
>
> > **Explanatory**
> >
> > For the purposes of WA H10D1 the applicable clauses from Part 7.4 are:
> >
> > - 7.4.3(b); and
> > - 7.4.4(1)(c), (2), (3) and (5); and
> > - 7.4.6; and
> > - 7.4.7.
> >
> > AS/NZS 3500.3:2021 provides a *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution* for the design and installation of eaves gutters however, it is without normative clauses for selecting their overflow measures. Part 7.4 of ABCB Housing Provisions which also provides a *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution* for the design and installation of eaves gutters includes overflow measures.
> >
> > Both AS/NZS 3500.3:2021 and Part 7.4 of the ABCB Housing Provisions require that eaves gutters are designed for a 5-minute duration rainfall intensity determined for an *annual exceedance probability* 5%, Part 7.4 of the ABCB Housing Provisions requiring that eaves gutter overflow provisions are determined for *annual exceedance probability* 1%.
> >
>

From **NCC 2025 Volume Two**, Class 1 and 10 buildings.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (WA) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This Part contains requirements for reducing water use.

## Objectives

<a id="h9o1"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9O1 Efficient use of water.
>
> The Objective is to reduce water demand by using water efficiently and minimising water wastage.
>

## Functional Statements

<a id="h9f1"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9F1 Drinking water demand.
>
> To reduce *drinking water* demand, a building is to be capable of using *drinking water* efficiently and preventing excessive loss of *drinking water*.
>

## Performance Requirements

<a id="h9p1"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9P1 Water use efficiency.
>
> A building must have features that, to the degree necessary, facilitate the efficient use of *drinking water* appropriate to—
>
> - (a) the geographic location of the building; and
> - (b) the available *drinking water* supply of the building; and
> - (c) the function and use of the building.
>
> > **Application**
> >
> > WA H9P1 only applies to Class 1 buildings, associated Class 10a buildings and *swimming pools* associated with a Class 1 building.
> >
>

<a id="h9p2"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9P2 Water loss prevention.
>
> A building, including any water holding structure, must have features that, to the degree necessary, prevent excessive loss of *drinking water* appropriate to—
>
> - (a) the geographic location of the building; and
> - (b) the available *drinking water* supply for the building; and
> - (c) the function and use of the building; and
> - (d) the effects of permanent features such as topography, structures and buildings.
>
> > **Application**
> >
> > WA H9P2 only applies to Class 1 buildings, associated Class 10a buildings and *swimming pools* associated with a Class 1 building.
> >
>

<a id="h9p3"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9P3 Heated water use efficiency.
>
> A building must have features that, to the degree necessary, facilitate the efficient use of heated water appropriate to—
>
> - (a) the geographic location of the building; and
> - (b) the available heated water supply for the building; and
> - (c) the function and use of the building.
>
> > **Application**
> >
> > WA H9P3 only applies to Class 1 buildings, associated Class 10a buildings and *swimming pools* associated with a Class 1 building.
> >
>

<a id="h9d1"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions.
>
> (1) *Performance Requirement* [WA H9P1](#h9p1) is satisfied for a building’s water use efficiency by complying with [WA H9D2](#h9d2).
>
> (2) *Performance Requirement* [WA H9P2](#h9p2) is satisfied for a building’s water loss prevention by complying with [WA H9D3](#h9d3).
>
> (3) *Performance Requirement* [WA H9P3](#h9p3) is satisfied for a building’s heated water use efficiency by complying with [WA H9D4](#h9d4).
>

<a id="h9d2"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9D2 Water use efficiency.
>
> (1) All tap fittings other than bath outlets and garden taps must be a minimum of 4 stars *WELS* rated.
>
> (2) All showerheads must be a minimum of 3 stars *WELS* rated.
>
> (3) All sanitary flushing systems must be a minimum of 4 star *WELS* rated dual flush.
>

<a id="h9d3"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9D3 Swimming pool covers and blankets.
>
> An outdoor private *swimming pool* or spa associated with a Class 1 building must be supplied with a cover, blanket or the like that—
>
> - (a) is designed to reduce water evaporation; and
> - (b) is compliant with Australian Standard AS 5348:2022 Pool Covers.
>

<a id="h9d4"></a>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces H9D4 Heated water use efficiency.
>
> (1) All internal heated water outlets (such as taps, showers and washing machine water supply fittings) must be connected to a heated water system or a recirculating heated water system with pipes installed and insulated in accordance with AS/NZS 3500: Plumbing and Drainage, Part 4 Heated Water Services.
>
> (2) The pipe from the heated water system or recirculating heated water system to the furthest heated water outlet must not be more than 20 m in length or 2 litres of internal volume.
>

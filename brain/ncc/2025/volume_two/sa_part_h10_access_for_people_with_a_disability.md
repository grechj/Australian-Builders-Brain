From **NCC 2025 Volume Two**, Class 1 and 10 buildings.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (SA) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This Part contains additional requirements for access for people with a disability.

## Objectives

<a id="h10o1"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces H10O1 Objective.
>
> Provide, as far as is reasonable, people with safe, equitable and dignified access to a degree necessary to—
>
> - (a) buildings; and
> - (b) the services and facilities within.
>
> > **Application**
> >
> > SA H10O1 applies to Class 1 buildings in developments of 20 or more dwellings.
> >
>

## Functional Statements

<a id="h10f1"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces H10F1 Equitable and dignified access.
>
> A building to the degree necessary is, as far as is reasonable, to provide safe, equitable and dignified access for people to the services and facilities within.
>
> > **Application**
> >
> > SA H10F1 applies to Class 1 buildings in developments of 20 or more dwellings.
> >
>

## Performance Requirements

<a id="h10p1"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces H10P1 Access for people with a disability.
>
> Buildings and immediate surrounds must have appropriate features to a degree necessary to enable people with a disability to safely and equitably—
>
> - (a) negotiate the route from the road boundary to and within the building using a wheelchair; and
> - (b) have access to spaces within the building, including facilities required under [H4P3](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h4_health_and_amenity.md#h4p3).
>
> > **Application**
> >
> > SA H10P1 applies to Class 1 buildings in developments of 20 or more dwellings.
> >
>

<a id="h10d1"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces H10D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions.
>
> Compliance with [SA H10D2](#h10d2) to [SA H10D4](#h10d4) for access for people with a disability satisfies *Performance Requirement* [SA H10P1](#h10p1).
>

<a id="h10d2"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces H10D2 Application of Part.
>
> This Part applies to certain Class 1 buildings where access is *required* under [SA H10D3](#h10d3).
>

<a id="h10d3"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces H10D3 Access to buildings.
>
> In developments consisting of 20 or more dwellings, access must be provide to and within one dwelling or 5% of the total number of dwellings, whichever is the greater.
>

<a id="h10d4"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces H10D4 Parts of buildings to be accessible.
>
> (1) Access for people with a disability must be provided from the entrance doorway to areas normally used by the occupants.
>
> (2) A path of travel providing *required* access must not include a stairway or other impediment which would prevent a person in a wheelchair using it.
>
> (3) Access, finishes and fittings must comply with the provisions of AS 1428.1.
>
> (4) In every Class 1 building to which access for people with a disability is *required*, one closet pan and washbasin and one shower must be provided for use by people with a disability.
>

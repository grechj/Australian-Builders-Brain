From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Energy efficiency.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (NSW, NT) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

## NT variation: NT deletes Part J2

This Part has deliberately been left blank.

For a Class 2 building and Class 4 part of a building, Section J is replaced with Section J of BCA 2009 (without NT variation). For Class 3 and Class 5-9 buildings, Section J of NCC 2025 does not apply and is replaced with Section J of NCC 2019 (without NT variation).

This Part sets out the application of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* in [Parts J3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md) to [J9](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md).

### J2D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

### J2D2 Application of Section J

> **NSW variation**: NSW replaces J2D2 Application of Section J.
>
> (1) For a Class 3 and 5 to 9 building, *Performance Requirement* [NSW J1P1](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1p1) is satisfied by complying with—
>
> - (a) [Part J4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j4_building_fabric.md), for the building *fabric*; and
> - (b) [Part J5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j5_building_sealing.md), for building sealing; and
> - (c) [Part J6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j6_air_conditioning_and_ventilation.md), for *air-conditioning* and ventilation; and
> - (d) [Part J7](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j7_artificial_lighting_and_power.md), for artificial lighting and power; and
> - (e) [Part J8](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j8_heated_water_supply_and_swimming_pool_and_spa_pool_plant.md), for heated water supply and *swimming pool* and spa pool plant; and
> - (f) [J9D3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d3), for facilities for energy monitoring; and
> - (g) [J9D5(2)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5) to [(4)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5), for onsite *renewable energy* systems.
>
> (2) For a Class 3 and Class 5 to 9 building, *Performance Requirement* [NSW J1P4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1p4) is satisfied by complying with [J9D4(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d4), [J9D4(2)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d4) and [J9D5(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5).
>

(1) For a Class 3 to 9 building, other than a Class 4 part of a building, *Performance Requirement* [J1P1](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1p1) is satisfied by complying with—

- (a) [Part J4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j4_building_fabric.md), for the building *fabric*; and
- (b) [Part J5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j5_building_sealing.md), for building sealing; and
- (c) [Part J6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j6_air_conditioning_and_ventilation.md), for *air-conditioning* and ventilation; and
- (d) [Part J7](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j7_artificial_lighting_and_power.md), for artificial lighting and power; and
- (e) [Part J8](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j8_heated_water_supply_and_swimming_pool_and_spa_pool_plant.md), for heated water supply and *swimming pool* and spa pool plant; and
- (f) [J9D3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d3), for facilities for energy monitoring; and
- (g) [J9D5(2)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5) to [(4)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5), for onsite *renewable energy* systems.

(2) For a Class 4 part of a building, *Performance Requirement* [J1P2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1p2) is satisfied by complying with—

- (a) [J3D3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d3), using *house energy rating software*; or
- (b) the following—
  - (1) [J3D4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d4), for ceiling fans; and
  - (2) [J3D5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d5), [J4D3(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j4_building_fabric.md#j4d3) to (4), and [Part J5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j5_building_sealing.md), for general thermal construction; and
  - (3) [J3D7](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d7), for roofs; and
  - (4) [J3D8](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d8) and [J3D11](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d11) to [J3D13](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d13), or [J3D9](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d9), for walls and *glazing*; and
  - (5) [J3D10](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d10), for floors.

(3) For a Class 4 part of a building, *Performance Requirement* [J1P3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1p3) is satisfied by complying with—

- (a) for the net equivalent energy usage—
  - (1) [J3D14](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d14), for a Class 4 part of a building with a total floor area not greater than 500 m<sup>2</sup>; or
  - (2) [J3D15](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j3_elemental_provisions_for_a_class_4_part_of_a_building.md#j3d15), using *house energy rating software*; and
- (b) [Part J6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j6_air_conditioning_and_ventilation.md), for *air-conditioning* and ventilation; and
- (c) [Part J7](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j7_artificial_lighting_and_power.md), for artificial lighting and power.

(4) For a Class 3 to 9 building, *Performance Requirement* [J1P4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1p4) is satisfied by complying with [J9D4(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d4), [J9D4(2)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d4) and [J9D5(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5).

## NSW variation: NSW deletes Part G7

This Part has deliberately been left blank. Part G7 does not apply in NSW as livable housing design requirements do not apply to sole-occupancy units in a Class 2 building in NSW.

This Part sets requirements for dwellings to include features that are designed to improve their accessibility and usability for occupants and visitors, including those with a mobility-related disability.

## Objectives

<a id="g7o1"></a>

### G7O1 Objective

The Objective of this Part is to ensure that housing is designed to meet the needs of the community, including older people and those with a mobility-related disability.

> **Application**
>
> G7O1 only applies to a Class 2 *sole-occupancy unit*.
>

## Functional Statements

<a id="g7f1"></a>

### G7F1 Livable housing design

A Class 2 *sole-occupancy unit* is to be designed such that it is—

- (a) easy to enter; and
- (b) easy to navigate in and around; and
- (c) capable of easy and cost effective adaptation; and
- (d) responsive to the changing needs of occupants.

> **Application**
>
> G7F1 only applies to a Class 2 *sole-occupancy unit*.
>

## Performance Requirements

<a id="g7p1"></a>

### G7P1 Livable housing design

Each *sole-occupancy unit* in a Class 2 building must be provided with—

- (a) at least one level and step-free entrance door into the *sole-occupancy unit* from an *accessible* part of the floor on which it is located; and
- (b) internal doors and corridors which facilitate unimpeded movement between spaces; and
- (c) a *sanitary compartment* that—
  - (1) facilitates independent access and use; and
  - (2) is located on the entry level of the *sole-occupancy unit*; and
- (d) a shower that facilitates independent access and use; and
- (e) the walls of a *sanitary compartment* referred to in (c), the shower referred to in (d) and a bath (where installed, other than a freestanding bath) constructed so as to facilitate future installation of grabrails, or the like, in a way that minimises the removal of existing wall linings.

> **Application**
>
> G7P1(a) only applies to a *sole-occupancy unit* that is located on an *accessible* floor.
>

<a id="g7d1"></a>

### G7D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

(1) Where a *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution* is proposed, *Performance Requirement* [G7P1](#g7p1) is satisfied by complying with [G7D2](#g7d2).

(2) Where a *Performance Solution* is proposed, the relevant *Performance Requirements* must be determined in accordance with [A2G2(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g2) and [A2G4(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g4) as applicable.

### G7D2 Livable housing design

> **SA variation**: SA replaces G7D2 Livable housing design.
>
> (1) Each *sole-occupancy unit* in a Class 2 building must comply with the ABCB Standard for Livable Housing Design, except for Part 1.
>
> (2) The *sanitary compartment* that is subject to Clause 4.1 of the ABCB Standard for Livable Housing Design may be located on either:
>
> - (a) the ground or entry level; or
> - (b) the lowest level containing a *habitable room* within the *sole-occupancy unit* of a Class 2 building.

Each *sole-occupancy unit* in a Class 2 building must comply with the ABCB Standard for Livable Housing Design, except for Part 1.

> **Explanatory**

### S48C1 Scope

This Specification contains the requirements for comparing sensible heating and cooling loads for each assessable zone in the proposed building with the corresponding zones in the reference building.

<a id="s48c2"></a>

### S48C2 Comparison of zone loads in proposed building and reference building

(1) The zone load is the sensible heating or cooling effect required to maintain the temperature in each zone within the range specified in [S34C3(8)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_34_modelling_parameters_for_j1v3.md#s34c3).

(2) The loads for each zone in the proposed building and the reference building must be calculated in accordance with the modelling parameters of [Specification 34](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_34_modelling_parameters_for_j1v3.md).

(3) A zone of the proposed building is assessable under [J1V3(1)(b)(iii)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3) to [(v)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3) if it has—

- (a) a 98<sup>th</sup> percentile *heating load* of at least 10 W/m<sup>2</sup>: or
- (b) a 98<sup>th</sup> percentile *cooling load* of at least 40 W/m<sup>2</sup>.

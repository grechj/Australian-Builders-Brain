From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Energy efficiency.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md).

### S34C1 Scope

This Specification contains the *required* modelling parameters for [J1V3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3).

<a id="s34c2"></a>

### S34C2 Reference building

The *annual greenhouse gas emissions* must be calculated for the *reference building* in accordance with the following:

- (a) The *reference building* must—
  - (1) subject to (b), (c) and (d), comply with *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* in [Parts J4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j4_building_fabric.md) to [J8](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j8_heated_water_supply_and_swimming_pool_and_spa_pool_plant.md); and
  - (2) have the minimum amount of mechanical ventilation *required* by [Part F6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f6_light_and_ventilation.md); and
  - (3) be provided with a solar photovoltaic system that complies with—
    - (1) [J9D5(2)(a)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5), [J9D5(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5) and [J9D5(4)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5) with PV panels modelled as parallel to the roof and assuming an average solar photovoltaic panel power rating of 225 W/m<sup>2</sup>; or
    - (2) [J9D5(2)(b)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5), [J9D5(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5) and [J9D5(4)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j9_energy_monitoring_and_on_site_distributed_energy_resources.md#j9d5).
- (b) The *external walls* must have a solar absorptance of 0.6.
- (c) The U-Value of the wall-glazing construction must be calculated according to [S37C3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_37_calculation_of_u_value_and_solar_admittance.md#s37c3).
- (d) The solar admittance of the wall-glazing construction must be calculated according to [S37C5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_37_calculation_of_u_value_and_solar_admittance.md#s37c5).
- (e) Where [J1V3(1)(b)(i)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3) or [J1V3(1)(b)(ii)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3) applies, the *air-conditioning* must—
  - (1) for 95% of the annual *hours of operation*, achieve dry bulb temperatures between—
    - (1) 18°C to 25°C for *conditioned spaces* with transitory occupancy; and
    - (2) subject to (ii), 21°C to 24°C in all other *conditioned spaces*; and
  - (2) if the proposed building has no mechanically provided cooling or has mixed mode cooling, have the same method of control and control set points for non-mechanical cooling as the proposed building.
- (f) The infiltration rate in each zone must be—
  - (1) 0.7 air changes per hour throughout all zones when there is no mechanically supplied *outdoor air*; and
  - (2) 0.35 air changes per hour throughout all zones at all other times.
- (g) The artificial lighting must achieve the *required* maximum *illumination power density* in [Part J7](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j7_artificial_lighting_and_power.md) without applying the control device adjustment factors.
- (h) *Minimum Energy Performance Standards* must be applied to *services* not covered by [Parts J6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j6_air_conditioning_and_ventilation.md) to [J8](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j8_heated_water_supply_and_swimming_pool_and_spa_pool_plant.md).

### S34C3 Proposed building and reference building

(1) The *annual greenhouse gas emissions* must be calculated for the proposed building and the *reference building* using the same—

- (a) *annual greenhouse gas emissions* calculation method; and
- (b) greenhouse gas emissions factors in accordance with (2); and
- (c) location in accordance with (3); and
- (d) climatic data, using a projection at year 2050 made under a Representative Concentration Pathway with an 8.5 W/m² increase in radiative forcing; and
- (e) adjacent structures and features; and
- (f) orientation; and
- (g) building form in accordance with (4); and
- (h) testing standards including for insulation, *glazing*, water heater and unitary *air-conditioning* equipment; and
- (i) *fabric* and *glazing* in accordance with (5); and
- (j) *services* in accordance with (6) and [S34C4](#s34c4); and
- (k) target for thermal comfort minimums as per [J1V3(1)(b)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3).

(2) For the purposes of (1)(b), greenhouse gas emissions factors must be based on the factors in [Table S34C3](#table_e2f658e1).

(3) For the purposes of (1)(c), location must be either—

- (a) the location where the building is to be constructed if appropriate climatic data is available; or
- (b) the nearest location with similar climatic conditions, for which climatic data is available.

(4) For the purposes of (1)(g), building form must include the same—

- (a) roof geometry; and
- (b) floor plan; and
- (c) number of *storeys*; and
- (d) location, extent and configuration of ground floors and basements; and
- (e) size and location of *glazing*; and
- (f) external doors.

(5) For the purposes of (1)(i), fabric and *glazing* must include the same—

- (a) thermal resistance of air films including any adjustment factors, moisture content of materials and the like; and
- (b) dimensions of external, internal and separating walls; and
- (c) internal shading devices, including their colour and their criteria for operation; and
- (d) location and extent of thermal mass.

(6) For the purposes of (1)(j), *services* must include the same—

- (a) range and type of *services* and energy sources including the same proportions and functions of air-cooled and water-cooled *air-conditioning* equipment; and
- (b) *air-conditioning* zones including—
  - (1) separate zones for each *storey*; and
  - (2) separate zones for each *sole-occupancy unit*; and
  - (3) in spaces larger than 20 m², separate perimeter zones, no deeper than 4 m adjacent to *external walls* with windows facing—
    - (1) within 45° of true north; and
    - (2) within 45° of true south; and
    - (3) within 45° of true east; and
    - (4) within 45° of true west; and
  - (4) separate centre zones comprising the remainder of each *sole-occupancy unit* within each *storey*; and
  - (5) the same method of calculating the temperature difference across *air-conditioning* zone boundaries; and
  - (6) the same method of combining zones with similar heating and cooling requirements, where necessary to simplify the simulation; and
- (c) floor coverings and furniture and fittings density; and
- (d) internal artificial lighting illumination levels; and
- (e) internal heat gains including people, lighting, appliances, meals and other electric power loads; and
- (f) *air-conditioning*, including chiller, fan and *boiler* equipment, system configuration; and
- (g) profiles for occupancy, *air-conditioning*, lighting and internal heat gains from people, hot meals, appliances, equipment and heated water supply systems based on—
  - (1) [Specification 35](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_35_modelling_profiles_for_j1v3.md); or
  - (2) *NABERS Energy* simulation requirements; or
  - (3) *Green Star* simulation requirements; or
  - (4) the actual building if—
    - (1) the operating hours per year are not less than 2,500; or
    - (2) the daily operating profiles are not listed in [Specification 35](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_35_modelling_profiles_for_j1v3.md); and
- (h) supply heated water temperature and rate of use; and
- (i) infiltration values, subject to (7); and
- (j) sequencing for water heaters, refrigeration chillers and heat rejection equipment such as cooling towers; and
- (k) where [J1V3(1)(b)(i)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3) applies, representation of clothing and metabolic rate of the occupants, and air speed from mechanical services; and
- (l) control of *air-conditioning*, in accordance with the requirements of [Part J6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j6_air_conditioning_and_ventilation.md), except if the controls for the proposed building are not adequately specified or cannot be simulated, the sample control specifications in Appendix B of AIRAH-DA28 must be used; and
- (m) environmental conditions such as ground reflectivity, form factors for topography and built environment, temperature of external bounding surfaces, air velocities across external surfaces, vegetation and the like; and
- (n) number, sizes, floors and traffic served by lifts and escalators; and
- (o) loss factors for solar PV installation.

(7) For the purposes of (6)(i), the intended building leakage at 50 Pa may be converted into a whole building infiltration value for the proposed building infiltration using Tables 4.16 to 4.24 of CIBSE Guide A if all of the following have been specified:

- (a) Additional sealing provisions to those *required* by [Part J5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j5_building_sealing.md).
- (b) An intended building leakage of less than 10 m<sup>3</sup>/hr.m<sup>2</sup> at 50 Pa.
- (c) Pressure testing to verify achievement of the intended building leakage.

(8) Where [J1V3(1)(b)(iii)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3), [(iv)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3) or [(v)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3) applies, the *air-conditioning* must—

- (a) subject to (b), for 95% of the annual hours of operation, achieve dry bulb temperatures between—
  - (1) 18°C to 25°C for *conditioned spaces* with transitory occupancy; and
  - (2) 21°C to 24°C in all other *conditioned spaces*; and
- (b) if the proposed building has no mechanically provided cooling or has mixed mode cooling, the proposed building and reference building must have the same method of control and control set points for non-mechanical cooling.

### S34C4 Services — proposed and reference building

For the modelling of *services* for the purposes of calculating *annual greenhouse gas emissions* —

- (a) system demand and response for all items of plant must be calculated on a not less frequent than hourly basis; and
- (b) energy usage of all items of plant must be calculated with allowances for—
  - (1) part load performance; and
  - (2) staging to meet system demand; and
- (c) energy usage of cooling plant must be calculated with allowances for—
  - (1) the impact of chilled water temperature on chiller efficiency; and
  - (2) the impact of condenser water temperature on water-cooled plant efficiency; and
  - (3) the impact of ambient temperature on air-cooled plant efficiency; and
  - (4) the energy use of primary pumps serving individual chillers; and
  - (5) the energy use of auxiliary equipment, including controls and oil heating for chillers; and
  - (6) thermal losses in the chilled water system; and
  - (7) the impact of chilled water temperature on thermal losses in the chilled water system; and
- (d) energy usage of water heating systems for space heating must be calculated with allowances for—
  - (1) the impact of water temperature on water heater efficiency; and
  - (2) the energy use of primary or feedwater pumps serving individual water heaters; and
  - (3) thermal losses in water heating systems; and
  - (4) the thermal mass of water heating systems, accounting for thermal losses during periods when the system is not operating; and
- (e) energy usage of fan and pump systems must be calculated with allowances for—
  - (1) the method of capacity regulation; and
  - (2) the use of either fixed or variable pressure control; and
- (f) energy usage of pump systems must be calculated with allowances for the system fixed static pressure head; and
- (g) energy usage of auxiliary equipment associated with co-generation and tri-generation systems, including pumps, cooling towers and jacket heaters, must be calculated; and
- (h) if energy usage for each is the same in the proposed building and the reference building, omission from the calculation for both the proposed building and reference building may be made for—
  - (1) heated water supply for food preparation and sanitary purposes; and
  - (2) lifts; and
  - (3) escalators; and
  - (4) electric vehicle charging; and
- (i) energy use of a lift in a building with more than one classification may be apportioned according to the number of *storeys* of the part for which the *annual greenhouse gas emissions* are being calculated.

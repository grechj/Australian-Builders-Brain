## NT variation: NT deletes Part J9

This Part has deliberately been left blank.

For a Class 2 building and Class 4 part of a building, Section J is replaced with Section J of BCA 2009 (without NT variation). For Class 3 and Class 5-9 buildings, Section J of NCC 2025 does not apply and is replaced with Section J of NCC 2019 (without NT variation).

This Part contains *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for compliance with Part J1. It sets out provisions that enable the monitoring of energy use (other than for billing purposes) and facilitate easy retrofit of renewable energy and electric vehicle charging equipment.

### J9D3 Facilities for energy monitoring

(1) A building or *sole-occupancy unit* with a *floor area* of more than 500 m<sup>2</sup> must have energy meters configured to record the time-of-use consumption of gas and electricity.

(2) A building with a *floor area* of more than 2 500 m<sup>2</sup> must have energy meters configured to enable individual time-of-use energy data recording, in accordance with (3), of—

- (a) *air-conditioning* plant including, where appropriate, heating plant, cooling plant and air handling fans; and
- (b) artificial lighting; and
- (c) appliance power; and
- (d) central hot water supply; and
- (e) internal transport devices including lifts, escalators and moving walkways where there is more than one serving the building; and
- (f) on-site *renewable energy* equipment; and
- (g) on-site electric vehicle charging equipment; and
- (h) on-site *battery systems*; and
- (i) other ancillary plant.

(3) Energy meters *required* by (2) must be interlinked by a communication system that collates the time-of-use energy data to a single interface monitoring system where it can be stored, analysed and reviewed.

(4) The provisions of (2) do not apply to energy meters serving individual *sole-occupancy units* with a *floor area* of less than 2 500 m<sup>2</sup>.

(5) Where a building has an Environmental Management Information System facility it must record, in addition to J9D3(2)(a) to (i), on a 5 minute time interval basis—

- (a) the energy use of any piece of equipment predicted to consume more than 5% of total building annual energy use; and
- (b) entering and leaving temperatures and energy use for each chiller, heat pump, electric heater or hot water heater used in *air-conditioning*; and
- (c) the temperature of all internal *air-conditioning* zones; and
- (d) the water flow and return temperature of chilled and heated water loops; and
- (e) the external temperature and humidity local to the building; and
- (f) measurement of internal carbon dioxide and car park pollutant levels, where these are used for controlling fan speed or outside air quantities; and
- (g) total instantaneous building electricity demand, recorded as an average over every 5 minutes of building operation; and
- (h) the start/stop command value and speed of *air-conditioning* and mechanical ventilation fans and pumps and associated system pressure measurement and set point; and
- (i) the damper command for economy cycle and ventilation dampers; and
- (j) the damper or valve command for energy reclaim equipment.

(6) The Environmental Management Information System in (5) must—

- (a) allow demand management control of the building’s—
  - (1) *renewable energy* system, including any battery energy storage system installed; and
  - (2) lighting system; and
  - (3) *air-conditioning* and ventilation system; and
  - (4) electric vehicle charging equipment; and
- (b) include an operational interface that enables remote users to visualise system data and operational status; and
- (c) allow for the visualisation of data in a graphical format, including the ability to compare individual data streams integrated over time periods and in time series; and
- (d) be able to provide bulk downloads of at least 2 years of historic time series data to users; and
- (e) allow scheduling, supervisory level setpoint override and modulation set-back of the *air-conditioning* and ventilation system; and
- (f) incorporate monitoring and verification functionality with the capability to—
  - (1) provide monitoring and verification of a change in energy use in accordance with the International Performance Measurement and Verification Protocol Options B and C; and
  - (2) automatically determine an energy performance baseline from historical building data and weather data; and
  - (3) compare building performance against the baseline, both numerically and graphically; and
  - (4) provide forecasts of the building’s *renewable energy* production.

(7) The requirements of (5) and (6) do not apply to services that consume less than 5% of the building’s total annual energy consumption.

> **Notes**
>
> An Environmental Management Information System that complies with J9D3(5) and (6) and records data as *required* by J9D3(2)(a) to (i) satisfies the requirement for ‘energy meters’ in J9D3(2).
>

### J9D4 Facilities for electric vehicle charging equipment

(1) Subject to (2), a *carpark* associated with a Class 3, 5, 6, 7b, 8 or 9 building must be provided with electrical distribution boards dedicated to electric vehicle charging—

- (a) in accordance with Table J9D4 in each *storey* of the carpark; and
- (b) labelled to indicate use for electric vehicle charging equipment.

(2) Electrical distribution boards dedicated to serving electric vehicle charging in a *carpark* must—

- (a) be fitted with a charging control system with the ability to manage and schedule charging of electric vehicles in response to total building demand; and
- (b) when associated with a Class 5 to 9 building, have capacity for each circuit to support an electric vehicle charger able to deliver a minimum of 12 kWh from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm daily; and
- (c) when associated with a Class 3 building, have capacity for each circuit to support an electric vehicle charger able to deliver a minimum of 48 kWh from 11:00 pm to 7:00 am daily; and
- (d) be sized to support the future installation of a 7 kW (32 A) type 2 electric vehicle charger in—
  - (1) 10% of car parking spaces associated with a Class 5 or 6 building; or
  - (2) 20% of car parking spaces associated with a Class 3, 7b, 8 or 9 building; and
- (e) contain space of at least 36 mm width of DIN rail per outgoing circuit for individual sub-circuit for individual sub-circuit electricity metering to record electricity use of electric vehicle charging equipment; and
- (f) be labelled to indicate the use of the space *required* by (f) is for the future installation of metering equipment.

> **Limitation**
>
> J9D4 does not apply to a stand alone Class 7a building.

### J9D5 Facilities for solar photovoltaic and battery systems

(1) The main electrical switchboard of a building must contain at least 2 empty three-phrase circuit breaker slots and 4 DIN rail spaces labelled to indicate the use of each space for a *battery system*.

(2) A building must be provided with an on-site solar photovoltaic system—

- (a) that covers 100% of the roof area, excluding areas—
  - (1) shaded for more than 25% of annual daylight hours; or
  - (2) where the roof pitch exceeds 45° relative to horizontal; or
  - (3) that are awnings; or
  - (4) that are car parks; or
  - (5) that are roof gardens; or
  - (6) that are terraces or other rooftop areas designated for occupation; or
  - (7) used for roof lights or translucent roofing for natural lighting or the like; or
  - (8) designated as a trafficable area, access for height safety systems or plant maintenance access or a *required* path of travel to an *exit*; or
  - (9) used for plant equipment, services installations or the like; or
  - (10) above exhaust air flows from heat rejection equipment or the like; or
  - (11) where provision of solar photovoltaic panel is impractical on account of panel size or mounting system limitations; or
- (b) with a peak system output rating per m<sup>2</sup> of building *conditioned space* not less than those specified in [Table J9D5a](#table_5905407a).

(3) In addition to (2), a building where gas is used for building services must be provided with further on-site photovoltaic system peak output capacity not less than the value specified in [Table J9D5b](#table_d88e368d).

(4) If the on-site solar photovoltaic system in (3) is placed on a building roof, the panels must—

- (a) not be shaded for more than 25% of daylight hours; and
- (b) not have a pitch of more than 45°.

> **Limitation**
>
> - (1) The requirements of J9D5(2) do not apply to a building—
>   - (1) where 100% of the effective roof area is shaded for more than 70% of annual daylight hours; or
>   - (2) provided with an on-site renewable energy generation system with a generation capacity at least equivalent to a solar photovoltaic system described under J9D5(2).
> - (2) The requirements of J9D5(1) do not apply to a building with *battery systems* installed.
>

> **Explanatory**
>
> Where compliance with J9D5 leads to the installation of an on-site solar photovoltaic system, existing network connection rules, governed by the Distribution Network Service Provider (DNSP), will apply. Depending on the size of the system and the amount of PV already installed in the local area, the DNSP may require a building to have certain features that help maintain local electricity grid stability. These could include options for a dynamic grid connection, demand response capability as per [J9D3(6)](#j9d3) for key energy using equipment, or on-site energy storage.

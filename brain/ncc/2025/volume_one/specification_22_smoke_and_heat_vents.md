From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Services and equipment.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). 

<a id="s22c1"></a>

### S22C1 Scope

This Specification contains requirements for *automatic* *smoke-and-heat vents*.

<a id="s22c2"></a>

### S22C2 Adoption of AS 2665

*Automatic* *smoke-and-heat vents* must be installed as a system complying with AS 2665 except that permanently open vents may form part of the smoke/heat venting system provided they comply with the relevant criteria for *automatic* *smoke-and-heat vents* in AS 2665.

<a id="s22c3"></a>

### S22C3 Controls

Where a *smoke-and-heat vent* system is installed to comply with [E2D14](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e2_smoke_hazard_management.md#e2d14) to [E2D20](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e2_smoke_hazard_management.md#e2d20), then, in addition to thermally released link operation, *smoke-and-heat vents* must also be initiated by smoke detection complying with [S20C6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_20_smoke_detection_and_alarm_systems.md#s20c6) and [S20C8](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_20_smoke_detection_and_alarm_systems.md#s20c8) and arranged in zones to match the smoke reservoirs.

From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Special use buildings.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (WA) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

WA Part I4 contains *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solutions* for *WA public buildings* that are additional to those contained in Sections D, F and I.

> **WA variation**: WA replaces I4D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions.
>
> (1) Where a *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution* is proposed, *Performance Requirements* [D1P1](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d1_access_and_egress.md#d1p1), [D1P2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d1_access_and_egress.md#d1p2), [D1P3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d1_access_and_egress.md#d1p3), [D1P4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d1_access_and_egress.md#d1p4), [D1P6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d1_access_and_egress.md#d1p6) and [F6P2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f6_light_and_ventilation.md#f6p2) are satisfied by complying with—
>
> - (a) [Part D2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d2_provision_for_escape.md), [Part D3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d3_construction_of_exits.md) and [F6D5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f6_light_and_ventilation.md#f6d5); and
> - (b) for additional requirements for *WA public buildings*, WA Part I4.
>
> (2) Where a *Performance Solution* is proposed, the relevant *Performance Requirements* must be determined in accordance with [A2G2(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g2) and [A2G4(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g4) as applicable.
>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces I4D2 Application of Part.
>
> The *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part apply to a *WA public building* or part of a building.
>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces I4D3 Additional exits.
>
> In a *WA public buildings*, each *storey* that accommodates more than 50 persons must have more than one *exit* from that *storey*.
>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces I4D4 Goings and risers.
>
> (1) Steps serving a *WA public building* must have *risers* not more than 180 mm and *goings* not less than 280 mm.
>
> (2) The requirements of (1) do not apply to steps in a *fire-isolated stairway*.
>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces I4D5 Handrails to ramps, flights and landings.
>
> (1) For a *WA public building*, handrails must be located along—
>
> - (a) both sides of a ramp or flight; and
> - (b) each side of a landing, except for that part of a side that is interrupted by a ramp or flight.
>
> (2) The requirements of (1) do not apply to—
>
> - (a) handrails referred to in [D3D23](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d3_construction_of_exits.md#d3d23); or
> - (b) handrails located within a *fire-isolated stairway* or *fire-isolated ramp*.
>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces I4D6 Artificial lighting.
>
> (1) Artificial lighting must be provided along the external path of travel (including steps, landings, ramps or paths) to a road or *open space* associated with each *exit* from a *WA public building*.
>
> (2) The artificial lighting system *required* by (1) must—
>
> - (a) provide a minimum illuminance of 1 lux at ground level; and
> - (b) be connected to circuits separate from those supplying lighting for foyers, entry porches, emergency escape passages or areas provided entry or egress to the *WA public building*; and
> - (c) be connected over two circuits where two or more lights are required; and
> - (d) have switches that are not able to be operated by members of the public.
>

> **WA variation**: WA replaces I4D7 Fixed seating.
>
> Where fixed seating in a Class 9b *WA public building* is arranged in more than one row—
>
> - (a) aisles must be provided on both sides of every row that contains more than 10 seats; and
> - (b) the number of seats in a row must not exceed 42.
>

From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Energy efficiency.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). 

<a id="s39c1"></a>

### S39C1 Scope

This Specification describes the thermal performance of sub-floor spaces and soil in direct contact with a floor for the purposes of calculating the *Total R-Value* of a floor.

<a id="s39c2"></a>

### S39C2 Sub-floor space and soil thermal performance

(1) [Table S39C2a](#table_959d876f) details the *R-Values* considered to be achieved by enclosed sub-floor spaces that are—

- (a) mechanically ventilated by not more than 1.5 air changes per hour; or
- (b) provided with not more than 150% of the aggregate sub-floor ventilation area *required* by [Part F1](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f1_water_management.md) and are not mechanically ventilated.

(2) [Table S39C2b](#table_f7def0a1) details the *R-Values* considered to be achieved by the soil for floors that are in direct contact with the ground.

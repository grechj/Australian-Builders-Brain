### S33C1 Scope

This Specification contains requirements that must be complied with in addition to the modelling requirements of [J1V1](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v1), [J1V2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v2) and [J1V3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3).

### S33C2 Additional requirements — general

(1) In addition to the modelling requirements for [J1V1](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v1), [J1V2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v2), and [J1V3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v3), a building must comply with—

### S33C3 Additional requirements — Green Star

Where not included in the building energy simulation to satisfy [J1V2(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j1_energy_efficiency_performance_requirements.md#j1v2), compliance must be achieved with—

- (a) for heating, cooling and ventilation equipment outside the scope of the *Green Star* model, [Part J6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j6_air_conditioning_and_ventilation.md); and
- (b) for artificial lighting outside the scope of the *Green Star* model, [Part J7](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j7_artificial_lighting_and_power.md).

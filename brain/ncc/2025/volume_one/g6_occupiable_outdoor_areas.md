From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Ancillary provisions.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). 

This Part provides additional *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for buildings that include an *occupiable outdoor area*.

<a id="g6d1"></a>

### G6D1 Application of Part

(1) The *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part apply to buildings containing an *occupiable outdoor area* in addition to the other *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of NCC Volume One.

(2) The *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part take precedence where there is a difference to the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of Sections C, D, E, F and G.

(3) Except for [G6D2](#g6d2), the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part do not apply to—

- (a) an *occupiable outdoor area* of a *sole-occupancy unit* in a Class 2 or 3 building, Class 9c building or Class 4 part of a building; or
- (b) an *occupiable outdoor area* with an area less than 10m<sup>2</sup>.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Part G6 contains *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* additional to those contained in Sections C, D, E, F and G for *occupiable outdoor areas*.
>

<a id="g6d2"></a>

### G6D2 Fire hazard properties

(1) Subject to (2), a lining, material or assembly in an *occupiable outdoor area* must comply with [C2D11](/ncc/2025/volume_one/c2_fire_resistance_and_stability.md#c2d11) as for an internal element.

(2) The following *fire hazard properties* of a lining, material or assembly in an *occupiable outdoor area* are not *required* to comply with [C2D11](/ncc/2025/volume_one/c2_fire_resistance_and_stability.md#c2d11):

- (a) *Average specific extinction area*.
- (b) *Smoke-Developed Index*.
- (c) *Smoke development rate*.
- (d) *Smoke growth rate index* (SMOGRA<sub>RC</sub>).

<a id="g6d3"></a>

### G6D3 Fire separation

For the purposes of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [C3D8](/ncc/2025/volume_one/c3_compartmentation_and_separation.md#c3d8), [C3D9](/ncc/2025/volume_one/c3_compartmentation_and_separation.md#c3d9) and [C3D10](/ncc/2025/volume_one/c3_compartmentation_and_separation.md#c3d10), a reference to a *storey* includes an *occupiable outdoor area*, however a *fire wall* cannot be used to separate an *occupiable outdoor area* into different *fire compartments*.

<a id="g6d4"></a>

### G6D4 Provision for escape

For the purposes of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [Part D2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d2_provision_for_escape.md), a reference to a *storey* or room includes an *occupiable outdoor area*.

<a id="g6d5"></a>

### G6D5 Construction of exits

For the purposes of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [Part D3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d3_construction_of_exits.md), a reference to a *storey* or room includes an *occupiable outdoor area*.

<a id="g6d6"></a>

### G6D6 Fire fighting equipment

Except for [S17C7(2)(a)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_17_fire_sprinkler_systems.md#s17c7), for the purposes of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [Part E1](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e1_fire_fighting_equipment.md), a reference to a *storey* includes an *occupiable outdoor area*.

> **Notes**
>
> An *occupiable outdoor area* is not a *storey* for the purposes of Schedule 1 of the NCC and therefore is not included in the determination of *rise in storeys*.
>

<a id="g6d7"></a>

### G6D7 Lift installations

For the purposes of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [Part E3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e3_lift_installations.md), a reference to a *storey* includes an *occupiable outdoor area*.

<a id="g6d8"></a>

### G6D8 Visibility in an emergency, exit signs and warning systems

For the purposes of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [Part E4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e4_visibility_in_an_emergency_exit_signs_and_warning_systems.md), a reference to a *storey* includes an *occupiable outdoor area*.

<a id="g6d9"></a>

### G6D9 Light and ventilation

For the purposes of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [F6D5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f6_light_and_ventilation.md#f6d5), [F6D9](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f6_light_and_ventilation.md#f6d9) and [F6D10](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f6_light_and_ventilation.md#f6d10), a reference to a room includes an *occupiable outdoor area*.

<a id="g6d10"></a>

### G6D10 Fire orders

For the purposes of the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [G4D8](/ncc/2025/volume_one/g4_construction_in_alpine_areas.md#g4d8), a reference to a *storey* includes an *occupiable outdoor area*.

### J3D2 Application of Part

The *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part apply to building elements forming the external building *fabric* and *domestic services* of a Class 4 part of a building.

### J3D3 Reducing heating and cooling loads of a Class 4 part of a building using house energy rating software

(1) The Class 4 part of a building must—

- (a) for reducing the heating or cooling loads—
  - (1) collectively achieve an average energy rating of not less than 7 stars, including the separate heating and cooling load limits; and
  - (2) individually achieve an energy rating of not less than 6 stars, including the separate heating and cooling load limits; and
- (b) for thermal breaks, comply with [J3D5](#j3d5); and
- (c) for compensating for a loss of ceiling insulation, other than where the *house energy rating software* has compensated for a loss of ceiling insulation, comply with [Table J3D7w](#table_ec13d480); and
- (d) for general thermal construction, comply with [J4D3(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j4_building_fabric.md#j4d3) to [J4D3(4)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j4_building_fabric.md#j4d3); and
- (e) for floor edge insulation, comply with [J3D10(3)](#j3d10), [J3D10(5)](#j3d10) and [J3D10(6)](#j3d10); and
- (f) for building sealing, comply with [Part J5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/j5_building_sealing.md).

(2) Energy ratings referred to in (1)(a)(i) and (ii) must be achieved using—

- (a) *house energy rating software*; and
- (b) the load limits specified in the ABCB Standard for NatHERS Heating and Cooling Load Limits.

### J3D4 Ceiling fans in a Class 4 part of a building

(1) Ceiling fans must be installed in accordance with [Table J3D4](#table_a563e03c) in—

- (a) *climate zones* 1, 2 and 3; and
- (b) *climate zone* 5 in New South Wales and Queensland.

(2) Ceiling fans *required* by (1) must—

- (a) be permanently installed; and

### J3D5 Roof thermal breaks of a Class 4 part of a building

(1) A metal-framed flat, skillion or cathedral roof that—

- (a) has metal sheet roofing directly fixed to metal purlins, metal rafters, metal battens or the like; and
- (b) does not have a ceiling lining or has a ceiling lining fixed directly to the metal purlins, metal rafters, metal battens or the like; and
- (c) has not achieved the *required* *Total R-Value* specified in [Table J3D7t](#table_94e96209), calculated using a method that accounts for the effects of thermal bridging,

must have a thermal break, consisting of a material with an *R-Value* of greater than or equal to R0.2, installed within the structure.

(2) The requirements of (1) do not apply to roofs constructed using insulated sandwich panels.

### J3D7 Roofs and ceilings of a Class 4 part of a building

(1) Roof and ceiling insulation must achieve the minimum *R-Value* —

- (a) in *climate zone* 1, in accordance with [Tables J3D7a](#table_47f0f340), [J3D7b](#table_b168963f) and [J3D7c](#table_040c8e5d) as applicable; and
- (b) in *climate zone* 2, in accordance with [Tables J3D7d](#table_c3622cfd), [J3D7e](#table_8467102b) and [J3D7f](#table_0bbc25fe) as applicable; and
- (c) in *climate zone* 3, in accordance with [Tables J3D7g](#table_b1c913db), [J3D7h](#table_eb065e2d) and [J3D7i](#table_9e0ae8f8) as applicable; and
- (d) in *climate zone* 4, in accordance with [Tables J3D7j](#table_f194dd51), [J3D7k](#table_baf3644d) and [J3D7l](#table_ebf8391e) as applicable; and
- (e) in *climate zone* 5, in accordance with [Tables J3D7m](#table_a3b46182), [J3D7n](#table_985791d8) and [J3D7o](#table_ac8df5c7) as applicable; and
- (f) in *climate zone* 6—
  - (1) R3.5; or
  - (2) if the roof contains *reflective insulation*, R3.0; and
- (g) in *climate zones* 7 and 8, in accordance with [Tables J3D7p](#table_4bae48b7), [J3D7q](#table_597c1e58) and [J3D7r](#table_04f52036) as applicable.

(2) *Reflective insulation* installed to comply with (1) must—

- (a) have a surface emittance of not more than 0.05; and
- (b) be adjacent to a roof space of not less than 20 mm; and
- (c) in *climate zones* 3 to 8, be downward facing.

(3) The thermal bridging in a metal-framed roof must be addressed as follows—

- (a) for a pitched roof with a horizontal ceiling—
  - (1) achieving the *Total R-Value* in [Table J3D7s](#table_c64c3eb4), calculated using a method that accounts for the effects of thermal bridging; or
  - (2) increasing the *R-Value* of the insulation between the ceiling frames by R0.5 more than the *R-Value* derived from (1); or
  - (3) adding a continuous ceiling insulation layer with a minimum *R-Value* of R0.13 above or below the ceiling joists or the bottom chords of the trusses; or
  - (4) achieving the *required* ceiling *R-Value* derived from (1) by stacking two layers of insulation immediately on top of each other, such that the top layer is orientated to cover the ceiling joists or bottom chord of the trusses and has an *R-Value* of at least R0.5; or
- (b) for a flat, skillion or cathedral roof—
  - (1) achieving the *Total R-Value* in [Table J3D7t](#table_94e96209), calculated using a method that accounts for the effects of thermal bridging; or
  - (2) complying with [Table J3D7u](#table_75f1be0d).

(4) Where [F8D5(1)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f8_condensation_management.md#f8d5) applies, continuous insulation placed above the *primary insulation layer* to mitigate thermal bridging must have a *vapour permeance* of not less than that of the *primary insulation layer*.

(5) Where, for operational or safety reasons, the area of ceiling insulation *required* is reduced, the loss of insulation must be compensated for in accordance with [Table J3D7w](#table_ec13d480).

(6) Where the ceiling insulation *required* by (1) to (5) has an *R-Value* —

- (a) greater than R3.0 and less than or equal to R4.5, it may be reduced to R3.0 within 450 mm of an *external wall*; or
- (b) greater than R4.5, it may be reduced to R3.0 within 450 mm of an *external wall*, provided all other *required* ceiling insulation is increased by R0.5.

(7) The requirements of (1) to (6) do not apply to roofs constructed using insulated sandwich panels.

(8) Roofs constructed using insulated sandwich panels must achieve the minimum *Total R-Value* in [Table J3D7x](#table_5f64a4fc).

(9) In *climate zones* 1 to 5, the solar absorptance of the upper surface of a roof must not be more than 0.64.

### J3D10 Floors of a Class 4 part of a building

(1) Where a Class 4 part of a building has a concrete floor above an unenclosed *carpark*, undercroft or the like, underfloor insulation must be installed with an *R-Value* at least—

- (a) in *climate zone* 2 and *climate zones* 5 to 8, R2.0; and
- (b) in *climate zones* 3 and 4, R1.5.

(2) Where a Class 4 part of a building has a concrete floor above an enclosed *carpark*, undercroft or the like, underfloor insulation must be installed with an *R-Value* at least—

- (a) in *climate zone* 2, R0.5; and
- (b) in *climate zones* 4 and 5, R1.0; and
- (c) in *climate zone* 6, R1.5; and
- (d) in *climate zones* 7 and 8, R2.0.

(3) A concrete slab-on-ground, including a waffle-pod slab, with an in-slab or in-screed heating or cooling system must have insulation with an *R-Value* at least 1.0 installed around the vertical edge of its perimeter.

(4) A concrete slab-on-ground, other than a waffle-pod slab, must be insulated in accordance with the following:

- (a) In *climate zones* 6 and 7—
  - (1) insulation with an *R-Value* of at least 0.64 must be installed around the vertical edge of its perimeter; and
  - (2) insulation with an *R-Value* of at least 0.64 must be installed underneath the slab.
- (b) In *climate zone* 8—
  - (1) insulation with an *R-Value* of at least 1.0 must be installed around the vertical edge of its perimeter; and
  - (2) insulation with an *R-Value* of at least 2.0 must be installed underneath the slab.

(5) Insulation *required* by (3), (4)(a)(i) and (4)(b)(i) must—

- (a) be *water resistant*; and
- (b) be continuous from the adjacent finished ground level—
  - (1) to a depth of not less than 300 mm; or
  - (2) for at least the full depth of the vertical edge of the concrete slab-on-ground.

### J3D12 External summer glazing of a Class 4 part of a building

(1) In *climate zones* 1 to 7, the aggregate solar heat gain of the *glazing* in each *storey*, including any *mezzanine*, of a Class 4 part of a building must—

- (a) not exceed the allowance resulting from multiplying the *floor area* of the *storey*, including any *mezzanine*, measured within the enclosing walls, by the constant C<sub>SHGC</sub> obtained from [Table J3D12a](#table_8bd9638d); and
- (b) be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

(A1×SHGC1×ES1×RS1×FS1×HS1)+(A2×SHGC2×ES2×RS2×FS2×HS2)+...

(2) In the formula at (1)(b)—

- (a) A1,2,etc= the area of each *glazing* element; and
- (b) SHGC1,2,etc= the *Total System SHGC* for each *glazing* element, not exceeding 0.7; and
- (c) ES1,2,etc= the summer exposure factor for each *glazing* element obtained from [Table J3D12b](#table_b0b76c1b), [J3D12c](#table_9ffdbbd4), [J3D12d](#table_809c5cc9), [J3D12e](#table_10599482), [J3D12f](#table_bedad862), [J3D12g](#table_8d15ff6f) or [J3D12h](#table_8a682a9c).
- (d) RS1,2,etc= the factor obtained from [Table J3D12i](#table_79d3bc65) or [Table J3D12j](#table_f77a73f9) for each *glazing* element located in a bedroom or room which is not a *conditioned space*; and
- (e) FS1,2,etc= the frame factor obtained from [Table J3D12i](#table_79d3bc65) or [Table J3D12j](#table_f77a73f9) for *glazing* element; and
- (f) HS1,2,etc= the floor factor obtained from [Table J3D12i](#table_79d3bc65) or [Table J3D12j](#table_f77a73f9) for each *glazing* element.

(3) For the purpose of J3D12—

- (a) orientation sectors must be determined in accordance with [Figure 13.3.2a](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_3_external_glazing.md#figure_b4dc0f0a) of the ABCB Housing Provisions; and
- (b) P/H must be determined in accordance with [Figure S37C7a](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_37_calculation_of_u_value_and_solar_admittance.md#figure_ca80c889); and
- (c) for P/H between those shown in [Tables J3D12b](#table_b0b76c1b), [J3D12c](#table_9ffdbbd4), [J3D12d](#table_809c5cc9), [J3D12e](#table_10599482), [J3D12f](#table_bedad862), [J3D12g](#table_8d15ff6f), [J3D12h](#table_8a682a9c), [J3D12i](#table_79d3bc65) and [J3D12j](#table_f77a73f9), either use the next highest P/H or interpolate.

### J3D13 Shading of a Class 4 part of a building

Where shading is *required* to comply with [J3D11](#j3d11) or [J3D12](#j3d12), it must—

- (a) be provided by an external permanent projection, such as a verandah, balcony, fixed canopy, eaves, shading hood or carport, which—
  - (1) extends horizontally on both sides of the *glazing* for a distance greater than or equal to the projection distance P in [Figure S37C7a](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_37_calculation_of_u_value_and_solar_admittance.md#figure_ca80c889); or
  - (2) provide the equivalent shading to (i) with a reveal or the like; or
- (b) be provided by an external shading device, such as a shutter, blind, vertical or horizontal building screen with blades, battens or slats, which—
  - (1) is capable of restricting at least 80% of the summer solar radiation; and
  - (2) if adjustable, is readily operated either manually, mechanically or electronically by the building occupants.

### J3D14 Net equivalent energy usage of a Class 4 part of a building

(1) The net equivalent energy usage of a Class 4 part of a building must not exceed the allowance determined in accordance with the following formula:

- (a) A×EF, where—
  - (1) A = the floor area factor obtained from multiplying the total floor area by the adjustment factor in [Table J3D14a](#table_ab767e99); and
  - (2) EF = the energy factor obtained from [Table J3D14b](#table_13656050).

(2) For the purposes of (1), the net equivalent energy usage must be calculated—

- (a) where the Class 4 part of a building is served by a dedicated heated water system, in accordance with (3); and
- (b) where the Class 4 part of a building is served by a centralised heated water system, in accordance with (4).

(3) The net equivalent energy usage of a Class 4 part of a building served by a dedicated heated water system must be determined in accordance with the following formula: (A×EE)+EP+ES−ER, where—

- (a) A = the floor area factor obtained from multiplying the total floor area by the adjustment factor in [Table J3D14a](#table_ab767e99); and
- (b) EE = the *main space conditioning* and *main water heater* efficiency factor obtained from the ABCB Standard for Whole-of-Home Efficiency Factors; and
- (c) EP = the *swimming pool* pump energy usage in (5); and
- (d) ES = the spa pump energy in (6); and
- (e) ER = the installed capacity of a on-site photovoltaics apportioned to the Class 4 part of a building (kW); and

(4) The net equivalent energy usage of a Class 4 part of a building served by a centralised heated water system must be determined in accordance with the following formula: (A×EE)+EP+ES+EC−ER−ERC, where—

- (a) A = the floor area factor obtained from multiplying the total floor area by the adjustment factor in [Table J3D14a](#table_ab767e99); and
- (b) EE = subject to (7), the *main space conditioning* and *main water heater* efficiency factor obtained from the ABCB Standard for Whole-of-Home Efficiency Factors; and
- (c) EP = the *swimming pool* pump energy usage in (5); and
- (d) ES = the spa pump energy usage in (6); and
- (e) EC = the centralised heated water system circulating pump energy usage in (8); and
- (f) ER = the installed capacity, in kW, of on-site photovoltaics apportioned to the Class 4 part of a building; and
- (g) ERC = the installed capacity, in kW, of on-site photovoltaics that directly offsets the energy consumption of circulating pumps for a centralised heated water system serving a Class 4 part of a building, where—
  - (1) this value does not exceed circulating pump energy usage (EC); and
  - (2) the on-site solar photovoltaics are directly connected to the same electrical supply as common area equipment in the building, including the circulating pumps.

(5) The *swimming pool* pump energy usage (E<sub>P</sub>) for a *swimming pool* dedicated solely to the Class 4 part of a building must be determined in accordance with the following formula: EP=V×FP/1000, where—

- (a) EP = the *swimming pool* pump energy usage; and
- (b) V = the volume of the *swimming pool* to the nearest 1000 litres; and
- (c) FP = the *swimming pool* pump factor in [Table 13.6.2c](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_6_whole_of_home_energy_usage.md#table_b2ee5799) of the ABCB Housing Provisions.

(6) The spa pump energy usage (E<sub>S</sub>) must be determined in accordance with the following formula: ES=V×FSB/100,where—

- (a) ES = the spa pump energy usage; and
- (b) V = the volume of the spa to the nearest 100 litres; and
- (c) FSB = the spa pump factor in [Table 13.6.2d](/ncc/2025/housing_provisions/13_6_whole_of_home_energy_usage.md#table_13339fee) of the ABCB Housing Provisions.

(7) The *main space conditioning* and *main water heater* efficiency factor for a Class 4 part of a building served by a centralised heated water system must be determined in accordance with the following—

- (a) for other than a solar boosted centralised heated water system, the equivalent water heater used to the determine the efficiency factor in the ABCB Standard for Whole-of-Home Efficiency Factors must be determined in accordance with [Table J3D14e](#table_478a15b1); and
- (b) for a solar boosted centralised heated water system, the efficiency factors must be determined in accordance with the following formula: EE=F1×P1+F2×P2, where—
  - (1) EE = the *main space conditioning* and *main water heater* efficiency factor; and
  - (2) F1 = the efficiency factor obtained from the ABCB Standard for Whole-of-Home Efficiency Factors using the equivalent water heater in [Table J3D14e](#table_478a15b1); and
  - (3) F2 = the efficiency factor obtained from the ABCB Standard for Whole-of-Home Efficiency Factors for—
    - (1) where the solar boosted centralised heated water system uses heat pump type heating as the auxiliary heating source, a heat pump (standard) water heater; and
    - (2) where the solar boosted centralised heated water system uses gas type heating as the auxiliary heating source, a gas storage water heater; and
    - (3) for all other types of solar boosted centralised heated water systems, an electric storage (standard) water heater; and
  - (4) P1 = the solar percentage factor determined in accordance with the following formula: P1=(AC/AS)/0.02, where—
    - (1) P1 = the solar percentage factor; and
    - (2) AC = the total area in m<sup>2</sup> of all solar thermal collectors connected to the heated water system; and
    - (3) AS = the total floor area in m<sup>2</sup> of all *sole-occupancy units* served by the heated water system.
  - (5) P2 = the non-solar percentage factor determined in accordance with the following formula: P2=1−P1, where—
    - (1) P2 = the non-solar percentage factor; and
    - (2) P1 = the solar percentage factor in (iv); and
  - (6) where the *main space conditioning* and *main water heater* efficiency factor for the heat pump F2 is less than that of the solar boosted system F1 then P1 must be set to 0 and P2 to 1; and
  - (7) P1 cannot be greater than 1.0.

(8) The circulating pump energy usage EC must be determined in accordance with the following formula: EC=LF×(CP+CS×NF), where—

- (a) EC = the circulating pump energy usage; and
- (b) LF = heated water load factor obtained from [Table J3D14c](#table_7d7a94c3); and
- (c) CP = the primary circulating pump energy factor obtained from [Table J3D14d](#table_bf87b151); and
- (d) CS = the secondary circulating pump energy factor obtained from [Table J3D14d](#table_bf87b151); and
- (e) NF = the factor for the number of *sole-occupancy units* served determined in accordance with the following formula: NF = 0.6 + (number of *sole-occupancy units* served - 100) x 0.00133; and
- (f) NF = cannot be greater than 1.0.

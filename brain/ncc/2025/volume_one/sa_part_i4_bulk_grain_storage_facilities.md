From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Special use buildings.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (SA) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This Part provides additional *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for Class 7b buildings which are *bulk grain storage facilities*.

<a id="i4d1"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D1 Application of Part.
>
> (1) The Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this Part apply to bulk grain storage facilities in South Australia.
>
> (2) The *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part take precedence where there is a difference to the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of Sections C, D, E and F.
>
> > **Explanatory**
> >
> > SA Part I4 contains *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* additional to those contained in Sections C, D, E and F for *bulk grain storage facilities*.
> >
>

<a id="i4d2"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D2 Type of construction required.
>
> The external walls of a *bulk grain storage facility* need not be of fire-resisting construction in accordance with [Table C2D2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/c2_fire_resistance_and_stability.md#table_7bde479d) if—
>
> - (a) the external walls are 3 m or more from an allotment boundary and more than 6 m from any other building on the same allotment, other than a Class 10 building; and
> - (b) a fire separation space of not less than 2 m is provided between *cell type silos*; and
> - (c) the external walls are of—
>   - (1) non-combustible construction; or
>   - (2) Type C construction.
>

<a id="i4d3"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D3 Compartmentation.
>
> Underground passageways in a *bulk grain storage facility* must be separated from other parts of the building by smoke proof walls and smoke-proof doors complying with [Specification 11](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_11_smoke_proof_walls_in_health_care_and_residential_care_buildings.md).
>

<a id="i4d4"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D4 Protection of openings.
>
> Where external stairs, ramps or ladders are used as an exit in a *bulk grain storage facility* —
>
> - (a) any window or door opening within 6 m of the stairway or ladder must be protected in accordance with [C4D5](/ncc/2025/volume_one/c4_protection_of_openings.md#c4d5); or
> - (b) the stairway, ramp or ladder must be enclosed for its full height above the lowest level of the window or door opening with non-combustible construction having an FRL of not less than 60/60/60.
>

<a id="i4d5"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D5 Provision for escape.
>
> (1) In addition to any horizontal exit, not less than 2 exits must be provided from—
>
> - (a) a *storage shed* in a *bulk grain storage facility* if the distance of travel to an exit is more than 150 m; and
> - (b) the gallery level in a cell type silo in a *bulk grain storage facility*.
>
> (2) A stairway or ramp serving as a required exit from a *bulk grain storage facility* is not required to be fire-isolated where there are no window or door openings within 3 m of the stairway, ramp or ladder.
>
> (3) Where required exits are spaced not more than 100 m apart in a *bulk grain storage facility*, the travel distance to an exit in a *cell type silo* or *storage shed* is not limited to [D2D5(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_one/d2_provision_for_escape.md#d2d5).
>
> (4) In a *bulk grain storage facility*, the distance from any point on the floor to a point of egress to a road or open space by way of a non-fire-isolated stairway, non-fire-isolated ramp or ladder is not limited.
>
> (5) A stair or ladder complying with AS 1657 may be used in a *bulk grain storage facility* as an alternative means of egress from a *cell type silo*.
>

<a id="i4d6"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D6 Fire hydrants and water supplies.
>
> (1) A *bulk grain storage facility* need not provide an on-site fire hydrant system in accordance with [E1D2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e1_fire_fighting_equipment.md#e1d2), where—
>
> - (a) street hydrants are available adjacent to the site that can supply water at not less than 5 litres per second; or
> - (b) a water supply system comprising single or multiple on-site water storage tanks with connections for use by the fire brigade and complying with the requirements of AS 2419.1 for water supplies and water storage is provided, except—
>   - (1) in lieu of a 4 hour water supply, an on0site water storage tank containing not less than 25,000 litres is provided within 90 m of a dry booster connection [Figure SA I4D6](#figure_27d60cfe); and
>   - (2) one dry riser is provided for each *cell type silo*, located in the middle of the longest side of the *bulk grain storage facility* and within 4 m of a required exit; and
>   - (3) all areas to be protected are within 60 m of a riser outlet, measured around obstacles; and
>   - (4) metal or high pressure plastic pipe can used for the dry riser; and
>   - (5) all pipes, valves and fittings for water storage tanks and connections must comply with section 5 and sections 8.2, 8.3, 8.6 and 8.7 of AS 2419.1 as relevant.
>
> (2) A dam may be regarded as a water storage tank if it complies with the requirements of section 5 of AS 2419.1 and the water level is maintained above the top of the highest *fire brigade* suction point at all times.
>
> (3) A hardstand area and vehicular approach from the site entrance, suitable for use by the fire brigade, must be provided adjacent to each water storage tank provided under (1)(b).
>

<a id="i4d7"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D7 Fire hose reels.
>
> [E1D3](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e1_fire_fighting_equipment.md#e1d3) does not apply to a *bulk grain storage facility*.
>

<a id="i4d8"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D8 Smoke venting.
>
> (1) In a *bulk grain storage facility*, suitable natural smoke venting by fixed vents must be provided at the top of elevator towers and overhead galleries in a *cell type silo*.
>
> (2) Smoke venting is not required in a *storage shed* of a *bulk grain storage facility*.
>

<a id="i4d9"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D9 Emergency lighting requirements.
>
> (1) An emergency lighting system complying with [E4D2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e4_visibility_in_an_emergency_exit_signs_and_warning_systems.md#e4d2) must be installed in a *bulk grain storage facility* except that—
>
> - (a) emergency lighting is not required at ground floor level in *cell type silos* or *storage sheds*; and
> - (b) the spacing of emergency lights can be increased to twice the distance required for Class 7 building.
>
> (2) An emergency lighting system complying with [E4D2](/ncc/2025/volume_one/e4_visibility_in_an_emergency_exit_signs_and_warning_systems.md#e4d2) must be installed in an underground passage in a *bulk grain storage facility*.
>

<a id="i4d10"></a>

> **SA variation**: SA replaces I4D10 Ventilation.
>
> In a *bulk grain storage facility*, a habitable room, office, shop, factory, workroom, sanitary compartment, bathroom, shower room, laundry and any other room occupied by a person for any purpose must have a ventilation system that provides one air change every six hours using openings that have a total area of the lesser of—
>
> - (a) 35 m<sup>2</sup>; or
> - (b) one percent of the total floor area.
>

From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Energy efficiency.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). 

<a id="s40c1"></a>

### S40C1 Scope

This Specification contains the requirements for lighting and power control devices including manual switches, time switches, demand-operated controls and daylight control devices.

<a id="s40c2"></a>

### S40C2 Manual switch

An artificial lighting manual switch must be located in a visible and easily accessed position—

- (a) in the room or space being switched; or
- (b) in an adjacent room or space from where at least 90% of the lighting being controlled is visible.

<a id="s40c3"></a>

### S40C3 Time switch

(1) A time switch must—

- (a) be capable of switching on and off electric power at variable pre-programmed times and on variable pre-programmed days; and
- (b) have the ability to program public holidays at least 1 year ahead.

(2) A time switch for internal lighting must be capable of being overridden by—

- (a) a demand-operated control; or
- (b) a manual switch.

(3) A time switch for external lighting must be capable of being configured to limit the period the system is switched on to between 30 minutes before sunset and 30 minutes after sunrise.

(4) A time switch for boiling water or chilled water storage units must be capable of being overridden for a maximum period of 2 hours outside normal operating hours.

<a id="s40c4"></a>

### S40C4 Demand-operated control

(1) A demand-operated control must—

- (a) activate artificial lighting upon detecting the presence or movement of an occupant; and
- (b) deactivate artificial lighting immediately or after a run-on period not more than 15 minutes after the space is vacated.

(2) A demand-operated control in a *fire-isolated stairway*, *fire-isolated passageway* or *fire-isolated ramp* must be configured so that a minimum of 30% of the full design illumination is provided when deactivation required by (1)(b) has occurred.

> **Notes**
>
> For the purposes of S40C4, demand-operated controls may detect the presence or movement of an occupant via a motion detector, key card, security system or the like.
>

<a id="s40c5"></a>

### S40C5 Daylight sensor and dynamic lighting control device

A daylight sensor and dynamic control device for artificial lighting must, in response to the measured level of available daylight in the range 50–1000 lux—

- (a) for high pressure discharge lights, switch lights on and off with a programmable delay and switching differential; and
- (b) for all other lights, dim continuously or step lighting output down in not less than 4 steps so that the lowest power setting is less than 50% of full power.

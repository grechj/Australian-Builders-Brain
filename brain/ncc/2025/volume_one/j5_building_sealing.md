## NT variation: NT deletes Part J5

This Part has deliberately been left blank.

For a Class 2 building and Class 4 part of a building, Section J is replaced with Section J of BCA 2009 (without NT variation). For Class 3 and Class 5-9 buildings, Section J of NCC 2025 does not apply and from is replaced with Section J of NCC 2019 (without NT variation).

This Part contains *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for compliance with Part J1. It sets out provisions for sealing a building to prevent air leakage. The provisions are intended to improve occupant thermal comfort by increasing *envelope* efficacy and reduce the energy consumption of any installed *air-conditioning* systems.

### J5D2 Application of Part

> **NSW variation**: NSW replaces J5D2 Application of Part.
>
> The *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part apply to elements forming the *envelope* of a Class 3 and Class 5 to 9 building, other than—
>
> - (a) a building in *climate zones* 1, 2, 3 and 5 where the only means of *air-conditioning* is by using an evaporative cooler; or
> - (b) a permanent building opening, in a space where a gas appliance is located, that is necessary for the safe operation of a gas appliance; or
> - (c) a building or space where the mechanical ventilation *required* by [Part F6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f6_light_and_ventilation.md) provides sufficient pressurisation to prevent infiltration; or
> - (d) parts of buildings that cannot be fully enclosed.
>

(1) The *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part apply to—

- (a) elements forming the *envelope* of a Class 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 or 9 building; and
- (b) the external building *fabric* of a Class 4 part of a building.

(2) Notwithstanding (1), the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part do not apply to—

- (a) a building in *climate zones* 1, 2, 3 or 5 where the only means of *air-conditioning* is by using an evaporative cooler; or
- (b) a permanent building opening, in a space where a gas appliance is located, that is necessary for the safe operation of a gas appliance; or
- (c) a building or space where the mechanical ventilation *required* by [Part F6](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f6_light_and_ventilation.md) provides sufficient pressurisation to prevent infiltration.

### J5D3 Chimneys and flues

The chimney or flue of an open solid-fuel burning appliance must be provided with a damper or flap that can be closed to seal the chimney or flue.

<a id="j5d4"></a>

### J5D4 Roof lights

A *roof light* must be sealed, or capable of being sealed, with—

- (a) an imperforate ceiling diffuser or the like installed at the ceiling or internal lining level; or
- (b) a weatherproof seal; or
- (c) a shutter system readily operated either manually, mechanically or electronically by the occupant.

<a id="j5d5"></a>

### J5D5 Windows and doors

(1) A door, openable *window* or the like must be sealed.

(2) The requirements of (1) do not apply to—

- (a) a *window* complying with AS 2047; or
- (b) a fire door or smoke door; or
- (c) a roller shutter door, roller shutter grille or other security door or device installed only for out-of-hours security.

(3) A seal to restrict air infiltration—

- (a) for the bottom edge of a door, must be a draft protection device; and
- (b) for the other edges of a door or the edges of an openable *window* or other such opening, may be a foam or rubber compression strip, fibrous seal or the like.

(4) An entrance to a building, must have an airlock, *self-closing* door, *rapid roller door*, revolving door or the like, other than—

- (a) where the room containing the entrance has a *floor area* of not more than 50 m<sup>2</sup>; or
- (b) where a café, restaurant, open front shop or the like has—
  - (1) an un-conditioned zone at least 3 m deep between the main entrance, including an open front, and a *conditioned space*; and
  - (2) at all other entrances to the café, restaurant, open front shop or the like, *self-closing* doors; or
- (c) where the entrance is a moveable external wall with the capability to open for the purpose of creating a temporary unconditioned space; or
- (d) where, for the safety of the building occupants or for the intended use of the facility, it is *required* to be left open to a temporary unconditioned space at an *early childhood centre*, preschool, *school*, or the like; or
- (e) where the entrance serves a Class 3 *sole-occupancy unit* or a Class 4 part of a building.

(5) A loading dock entrance, if leading to a *conditioned space*, must be fitted with a *rapid roller door* or the like.

<a id="j5d6"></a>

### J5D6 Exhaust fans

An exhaust fan must be fitted with a sealing device such as a self-closing damper or the like unless it is necessary for the fan to operate continuously.

<a id="j5d7"></a>

### J5D7 Construction of roofs, ceilings, walls and floors

(1) Ceilings, walls, floors and any opening such as a *window* frame, door frame, *roof light* frame, ventilation opening frame or the like must be constructed to minimise air leakage.

(2) Construction *required* by (1) must be—

- (a) enclosed by internal lining systems that are close fitting at ceiling, wall and floor junctions; or
- (b) sealed at junctions and penetrations with—
  - (1) close fitting architrave, skirting or cornice; or
  - (2) expanding foam, rubber compressible strip, caulking or the like.

(3) The requirements of (1) do not apply to openings, grilles, louvres or the like *required* for smoke hazard management.

<a id="j5d8"></a>

### J5D8 Evaporative coolers

An evaporative cooler must be fitted with a self-closing damper or the like in *climate zones* 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8.

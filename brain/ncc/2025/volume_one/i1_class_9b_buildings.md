From **NCC 2025 Volume One**, Special use buildings.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (NSW) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This Part provides additional *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* for certain types of Class 9b buildings where large numbers of people assemble and which contain a *stage* and *backstage* area.

<a id="i1d1"></a>

### I1D1 Application of Part

> **NSW variation**: NSW replaces I1D1 Application of Part.
>
> (1) For a Class 9b building or part of a building that is not an *entertainment venue* —
>
> - (a) the *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of [Part I1]() apply to every enclosed Class 9b building or part of a building which—
>   - (1) is a *school* assembly, church or community hall with a *stage* and any *backstage* area with a total *floor area* of more than 300 m<sup>2</sup>; or
>   - (2) otherwise, has a *stage* and any *backstage* area with a total *floor area* of more than 200 m<sup>2</sup>; or
>   - (3) has a *stage* with an associated rigging loft; and
> - (b) notwithstanding (1)(a)—
>   - (1) [I1D4](#i1d4) applies to every open or enclosed Class 9b building; and
>   - (2) [I1D7](#i1d7) applies to every enclosed Class 9b building.
>
> (2) For a Class 9b building that is an *entertainment venue*, [NSW Part I4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/nsw_part_i4_entertainment_venues_other_than_temporary_structures_and_drive_in_theatres.md) applies in replacement of [Part I1]().
>

(1) The *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* of this Part apply to every enclosed Class 9b building or part of a building which—

- (a) is a *school* assembly, church or community hall with a *stage* and any *backstage* area with a total *floor area* of more than 300 m<sup>2</sup>; or
- (b) otherwise, has a *stage* and any *backstage* area with a total *floor area* of more than 200 m<sup>2</sup>; or
- (c) has a *stage* with an associated rigging loft.

(2) Notwithstanding (1)—

- (a) [I1D4](#i1d4) applies to every open or enclosed Class 9b building; and
- (b) [I1D7](#i1d7) applies to every enclosed Class 9b building.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Part I1 contains *Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions* additional to those contained in Sections C, D and E for Class 9b buildings.
>

<a id="i1d2"></a>

### I1D2 Separation

A theatre, public hall or the like must—

- (a) have a sprinkler system (other than a FPAA101D or FPAA101H system) complying with [Specification 17](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_17_fire_sprinkler_systems.md); or
- (b) have the *stage*, *backstage* area and accessible under *stage* area separated from the audience by a proscenium wall in accordance with [I1D3](#i1d3).

<a id="i1d3"></a>

### I1D3 Proscenium wall construction

A proscenium wall must comply with [Specification 32](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_32_construction_of_proscenium_walls.md).

<a id="i1d4"></a>

### I1D4 Seating area

In a seating area—

- (a) the gradient of the floor surface must not be steeper than 1 in 8, or the floor must be stepped so that—
  - (1) a line joining the nosings of consecutive steps does not exceed an angle of 30° to the horizontal; and
  - (2) the height of each step in the stepped floor is not more than 600 mm; and
  - (3) the height of any opening in such a step is not more than 125 mm; and
- (b) if an aisle divides the stepped floor and the difference in level between any 2 consecutive steps—
  - (1) exceeds 230 mm but not 400 mm — an intermediate step must be provided in the aisle; and
  - (2) exceeds 400 mm — 2 equally spaced intermediate steps must be provided in the aisle; and
  - (3) the going of intermediate steps must be not less than 270 mm and such as to provide as nearly as practicable equal treads throughout the length of the aisle; and
- (c) the clearance between rows of fixed seats used for viewing performing arts, sport or recreational activities must be not less than—
  - (1) 300 mm if the distance to an aisle is not more than 3.5 m; or
  - (2) 500 mm if the distance to an aisle is more than 3.5 m.

<a id="i1d5"></a>

### I1D5 Exits from stages

(1) The path of travel to an *exit* from a *stage* or performing area must not pass through the proscenium wall if the *stage* area is separated from the audience area with a proscenium wall.

(2) *Required* *exits* from *backstage* and under-*stage* areas must be independent of those provided for the audience area.

<a id="i1d6"></a>

### I1D6 Access to platforms and lofts

A stairway that provides access to a service platform, rigging loft, or the like, must comply with AS 1657.

<a id="i1d7"></a>

### I1D7 Aisle lights

In every enclosed Class 9b building, where in any part of the auditorium, the general lighting is dimmed or extinguished during public occupation and the floor is stepped or is inclined at a slope steeper than 1 in 12, aisle lights must be provided to illuminate the full length of the aisle and tread of each step.

From **NCC 2025 Volume Three**, Water services.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (NSW, VIC) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This Part sets out the requirements for any part of a *non-drinking water* service of a property. It covers from the *point of connection* to the points of discharge.

Rainwater is not classified as a *non-drinking water* service.

## Objectives

<a id="b3o1"></a>

### B3O1 Objective

The Objective of this Part is to—

- (a) safeguard people from illness, injury or *loss* (including *loss* of *amenity*) due to the failure of a *non-drinking water* installation; and
- (b) ensure that a *non-drinking water* installation is suitable; and
- (c) conserve water and energy; and
- (d) safeguard the environment; and
- (e) safeguard public and private infrastructure; and
- (f) ensure that a *non-drinking water* installation throughout its serviceable life will continue to satisfy the requirements of Objectives (a) to (e).

## Functional Statements

<a id="b3f1"></a>

### B3F1 Non-drinking water supply

Sanitary fixtures, sanitary appliances and supply outlets provided with *non-drinking water* must be adequate.

<a id="b3f2"></a>

### B3F2 Non-drinking water installations

*Non-drinking water* must be supplied through *plumbing* installations in a way that avoids the likelihood of inadvertent contamination of any *drinking water* service, minimise any adverse impact on building occupants, the *Network Utility Operator’s* infrastructure, property and the environment.

## Performance Requirements

<a id="b3p1"></a>

### B3P1 Non-drinking water supply

A *non-drinking water* service must not have a *cross-connection* with a *drinking water* service.

<a id="b3p2"></a>

### B3P2 Identification

(1) Pipes, outlets, fittings, storage and holding tanks that are part of a *non-drinking water* service must be clearly identified.

(2) A *non-drinking water* service must only be connected to outlets clearly identified for non-drinking use.

<a id="b3p3"></a>

### B3P3 Velocity

Water velocity within *non-drinking water* service pipework must not exceed 3 m/s for no less than 99% of the time that water is required during the peak hour.

> **Explanatory**
>
> During the hour of heaviest usage in reticulated non-drinking services, a water velocity of 3 m/s must not be exceeded for 99% of the time that any downstream fixtures are in use.
>

<a id="b3p4"></a>

### B3P4 Access

Access must be provided to components of a *non-drinking water* service that require maintenance or replacement, including mechanical devices and controls.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Access to mechanical components may require removal of an access panel, cover, door, or similar obstruction.
>

<a id="b3p5"></a>

### B3P5 Isolation

Means of isolation must be provided to a *non-drinking water* service and components that require testing, maintenance and replacement including mechanical devices, appliances and controls.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Isolation valves are a means of providing isolation.
>

<a id="b3p6"></a>

### B3P6 Pressure

The points of discharge for a *non-drinking water* service must—

- (a) have—
  - (1) a working pressure of not less than 50 kPa; and
  - (2) a static pressure within the building of not more than 500 kPa: or
- (b) have water pressures suitable for the correct functioning of the fixture or appliance where water pressures outside of (a)(i) and (a)(ii) are required.

<a id="b3p7"></a>

### B3P7 Uncontrolled discharge

Any failure or *uncontrolled discharge* from a *non-drinking water* service must be avoided.

<a id="b3p8"></a>

### B3P8 Water efficiency

Where a *non-drinking water* service is used for flushing of toilets or urinals, efficient use of *non-drinking water* must be achieved by—

- (a) incorporation of—
  - (1) a cistern or flushing device for a urinal, to a flush volume of not more than 2.5 litres for each—
    - (1) single urinal stall; or
    - (2) 600mm length of a continuous urinal wall; and
  - (2) a dual flush cistern or flushing valve that is connected to a water closet pan, to a flush volume of not more than—
    - (1) 6 and 3 litres; or
    - (2) 4.5 and 3 litres; or
- (b) water saving measures equivalent to or greater than those described in (a).

> **Application**
>
> The flush volumes of B3P8 may be within a tolerance of—
>
> - (a) ±0.5 litres for the full flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> - (b) ±0.5 litres for the reduced flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> - (c) ±0.2 litres for a 4.5/3 litre cistern.
>

> **Exemption**
>
> The requirements of B3P8 do not apply to a vacuum *drainage* system.
>

<a id="b3d1"></a>

### B3D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

(1) Where a *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution* is proposed, *Performance Requirements* [B3P1](#b3p1) to [B3P8](#b3p8) are satisfied by complying with [B3D2](#b3d2) and [B3D5](#b3d5).

(2) Where a *Performance Solution* is proposed, the relevant *Performance Requirements* must be determined in accordance with [A2G2(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g2) and [A2G4(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g4) as applicable.

<a id="b3d2"></a>

### B3D2 Distribution of non-drinking water

A *non-drinking water* service must not be connected to any outlet that supplies water for—

- (a) human consumption; or
- (b) food preparation; or
- (c) food utensil washing; or
- (d) personal hygiene.

<a id="b3d3"></a>

### B3D3 General requirements

> **NSW variation**: NSW replaces B3D3 General requirements.
>
> A *non-drinking water* service must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1, with the following additions to clause 10.3—
>
> - (a) Clause 10.3(d): Top up from a *drinking water* supply shall be by an indirect trickle top up with a visible air gap external to the tank.
> - (b) 10.3(e): There shall be no connection between treated greywater systems and *drinking water*, rainwater or other sources of supply.
>

> **VIC variation**: VIC replaces B3D3 General requirements.
>
> A *non-drinking water* service must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1, subject to the following:
>
> - (a) Substitute clause 9.7.1(a) as follows: 9.7.1(a) be of a type that has a removable handle.
> - (b) Substitute the text of clause 9.7.1(c) as follows: 9.7.1(c) have a non-standard inlet connecting thread and a standard hose connection outlet.
>
> > **Explanatory**
> >
> > Requirements for a non-drinking water service used for fire-fighting purposes are contained in Part B4.
> >
>

A *non-drinking water* service must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Requirements for a *non-drinking water* service used for fire-fighting purposes are contained in [Part B4](/ncc/2025/volume_three/b4_fire_fighting_water_services.md).
>

<a id="b3d4"></a>

### B3D4 Bushfire prone areas

A *non-drinking water* service in *designated bushfire prone areas* must be in accordance with AS 3959.

### B3D5 Water efficiency

> **NSW variation**: NSW replaces B3D5 Water efficiency.
>
> (1) Cisterns or flushing devices for water closets must—
>
> - (a) Have a dual flushing mechanism; and
> - (b) Be a minimum 3 Star WELS rating discharging not more than 6 litres or 4.5 litre for a full flush.
>
> (2) Cisterns or flushing devices for urinals must—
>
> - (a) Be a minimum 2 Star WELS rating discharging a volume of not more than 2.5 litres of each—
>   - (1) Single urinal stall; or
>   - (2) 600mm length of continuous urinal wall; and
> - (b) Not be set-cycled or activated by any method other than manual or use activation.
>
> > **Explanatory**
> >
> > In New South Wales, cisterns and flushing devices installed within new Class 1 and 2 Buildings are subject to the water efficiency targets regulated under BASIX
> >
>
> > **Application**
> >
> > The flush volumes of B3D5(2) and B3D5(3) may be within a tolerance of—
> >
> > - (a) ±0.5 litres for the full flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> > - (b) +0.5 litres for the reduced flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> > - (c) ±0.2 litres for a 4.5/3 litre cistern.
> >
>
> > **Explanatory**
> >
> > - Prior to installing a programmed solenoid operated flushing system, further advice should be sought from the authority having jurisdiction.
> > - Where sensor control is used for urinal flushing, sensors should be located to avoid unnecessary ‘nuisance’ flushing triggered by pedestrian traffic.
> > - AS/NZS 6400 Water Efficient Products - Rating and labelling, provides the basis for the rating and labelling of a range of *products* under the mandatory Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (WELS) Scheme.
> > - B1D2 outlines the minimum level of water efficiency for certain *plumbing* *products* and fixtures, however the adoption of *products* and technologies of greater efficiency should be considered.
> >
>
> > **Exemption**
> >
> > - (1) The requirements of B3D5(2) and B3D5(3) do not apply to a vacuum *drainage* system.
> > - (2) The requirements of B3D5(3)(b) do not apply to a programmed solenoid operated flushing system if programmed to shut down during extended periods of non-occupancy of a building.
> >

(1) Cisterns or flushing devices for water closets must—

- (a) have a dual flushing mechanism; and
- (b) be a minimum 3 Star WELS rating, discharging not more than 6 litres or 4.5 litres for a full flush.

(2) Cisterns or flushing devices for urinals must—

- (a) be a minimum 2 Star WELS rating, discharging a volume of not more than 2.5 litres for each—
  - (1) single urinal stall; or
  - (2) 600mm length of continuous urinal wall; and
- (b) not be set-cycled or activated by any method other than manual or use activation.

> **Application**
>
> The flush volumes of B3D5(2) and B3D5(3) may be within a tolerance of—
>
> - (a) ±0.5 litres for the full flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> - (b) +0.5 litres for the reduced flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> - (c) ±0.2 litres for a 4.5/3 litre cistern.
>

> **Exemption**
>
> - (1) The requirements of B3D5(2) and B3D5(3) do not apply to a vacuum *drainage* system.
> - (2) The requirements of B3D5(3)(b) do not apply to a programmed solenoid operated flushing system if programmed to shut down during extended periods of non-occupancy of a building.
>

> **Explanatory**
>
> - Prior to installing a programmed solenoid operated flushing system, further advice should be sought from the Authority having jurisdiction.
> - Where sensor control is used for urinal flushing, sensors should be located to avoid unnecessary ‘nuisance’ flushing triggered by pedestrian traffic.
> - AS/NZS 6400 Water Efficient Products - Rating and labelling, provides the basis for the rating and labelling of a range of *products* under the mandatory Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (WELS) Scheme.
> - B3D5 Water efficiency outlines the minimum level of water efficiency for certain *plumbing* *products* and fixtures, however the adoption of *products* and technologies of greater efficiency should be considered.
>

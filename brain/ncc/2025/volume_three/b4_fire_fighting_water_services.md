## Part B4 Fire-fighting water services

Part B4 does not apply in Queensland.

> **Notes**
>
> This Part does not apply in Queensland. Fire-fighting water services are required under the Queensland Building Act 1975.
>

## Part B4 Fire-fighting water services

In New South Wales Fire-fighting water services are not the responsibility of the plumbing regulator.

In New South Wales compliance is not required with the national provisions of B4 as fire-fighting water services are regulated under the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 and Environmental Planning and Assessment Regulation 2021 and Environmental Planning and Assessment (Development Certification and Fire Safety) Regulation 2021 which references the Building Code of Australia.

This Part sets out requirements for any part of a fire-fighting water service from the *point of connection* or other acceptable source(s) of supply to the fire-fighting equipment, including hydrants, hose reels, sprinkler services, *automatic* fire suppression systems and wall drencher systems.

## Objectives

<a id="b4o1"></a>

### B4O1 Objective

The Objective of this Part is to—

- (a) safeguard people from illness, injury or *loss* (including *loss* of *amenity*) due to the failure of a fire-fighting water installation; and
- (b) ensure that a fire-fighting water installation is suitable; and
- (c) conserve water and energy; and
- (d) safeguard the environment; and
- (e) safeguard public and private infrastructure; and
- (f) ensure that a fire-fighting water installation is designed and is capable of being maintained so that throughout its serviceable life it will continue to satisfy Objectives (a) to (e).

## Functional Statements

<a id="b4f1"></a>

### B4F1 Fire-fighting water supply

Fire-fighting equipment must be provided with adequate water for its intended purpose.

## Performance Requirements

<a id="b4p1"></a>

### B4P1 Flow rate and pressure

Water flow rate and pressure to fire-fighting equipment and services must be adequate for the correct functioning of fire-fighting equipment or service and must be assessed based on a minimum 95th percentile flow rate and pressure of the water supply.

<a id="b4p2"></a>

### B4P2 Access

Access must be provided to components of a fire-fighting water service that require testing, maintenance or replacement, including mechanical devices and controls.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Access to mechanical components may require removal of an access panel, cover, door, or similar obstruction.
>

<a id="b4p3"></a>

### B4P3 Isolation

(1) A means of isolation must be provided to a fire-fighting water service and components that require testing, maintenance or replacement, including mechanical devices and controls.

(2) Adequate drainage must be provided for flow testing of fire-fighting water services.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Isolation valves are a means of providing isolation.
>

<a id="b4p4"></a>

### B4P4 Fire-fighting water storage

Water storage supplying fire-fighting systems must be sized suitably for the level of risk and supply arrangements.

<a id="b4p5"></a>

### B4P5 Uncontrolled discharge

Any failure or *uncontrolled discharge* from a fire-fighting water service must be avoided.

<a id="b4d1"></a>

### B4D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

(1) Where a *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution* is proposed, *Performance Requirements* [B4P1](#b4p1) to [B4P4](#b4p4) are satisfied by complying with [B4D2](#b4d2) to [B4D6](#b4d6).

(2) Where a *Performance Solution* is proposed, the relevant *Performance Requirements* must be determined in accordance with [A2G2(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g2) and [A2G4(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g4) as applicable.

<a id="b4d3"></a>

### B4D3 Fire sprinkler systems

(1) *Automatic* fire sprinkler systems must be in accordance with—

- (a) AS 2118.1 for general systems; or
- (b) AS 2118.4 for accommodation buildings not exceeding four *storeys*; or
- (c) AS 2118.5 for home fire sprinkler systems.

(2) *Automatic* fire sprinkler systems for combined sprinkler and hydrant systems in multi-storey buildings must be in accordance with—

- (a) AS 2118.1 for general systems; or
- (b) AS 2118.6 for multi-storey buildings; or
- (c) FPAA101H for buildings which are less than 25m in *effective height* and contain [Class 2](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g3) and [Class 3](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g4) parts.

(3) Notwithstanding (2)(b), plastic pipes and fittings must not be used above-ground in combined sprinkler and hydrant systems.

> **Explanatory**
>
> For residential fire sprinklers to FPAA101D Automatic Fire Sprinkler System Design - Drinking water supply, see [Part B1](/ncc/2025/volume_three/b1_cold_water_services.md) Cold water services.
>

<a id="b4d4"></a>

### B4D4 Fire hydrants

Fire hydrants must be in accordance with AS 2419.1.

<a id="b4d5"></a>

### B4D5 Fire hose reels

Fire hose reels must be in accordance with AS 2441.

<a id="b4d6"></a>

### B4D6 Bushfire prone areas

Fire-fighting water services in *designated bushfire prone areas* must be in accordance with AS 3959.

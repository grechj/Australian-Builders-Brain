From **NCC 2025 Volume Three**, Excessive noise.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (NT) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This Part sets out the requirements to prevent excessive noise being generated from a *plumbing* and *drainage* system that could cause illness or *loss* of *amenity* to occupants in a building.

## Objectives

<a id="d1o1"></a>

### D1O1 Objective

The Objective of this Part is to—

- (a) safeguard people from illness, injury or *loss* (including loss of *amenity*) due to excessive noise of a *plumbing* and *drainage* system; and
- (b) ensure that a *plumbing* and *drainage* system installation throughout its serviceable life will continue to satisfy the requirements of Objective (a).

## Functional Statements

<a id="d1f1"></a>

### D1F1 Excessive noise

A *plumbing* and *drainage* system must operate in a way that avoids the likelihood of excessive noise of any part of the *plumbing* and *drainage* system, and minimises any adverse impact on building occupants.

## Performance Requirements

<a id="d1p1"></a>

### D1P1 Undue noise

A *plumbing* and *drainage* system must not create undue noise.

<a id="d1p2"></a>

### D1P2 Excessive noise

(1) A *plumbing* and *drainage* system must reduce the transmission of airborne and/or impact generated sound which may cause illness or *loss* of *amenity* to occupants.

(2) The *required* sound insulation of a floor or wall must not be compromised by the incorporation or penetration of a *plumbing* or *drainage* system.

> **Application**
>
> D1P2 only applies to a *plumbing* or *drainage* system that is located in—
>
> - (a) a *separating wall* of a [Class 1](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g2) building; or
> - (b) a [Class 2](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g3), [Class 3](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g4) or [Class 9c](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g10) building that is *required* to be sound rated.
>

> **Notes**
>
> - (1) [Part F7](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f7_sound_transmission_and_insulation.md) in Volume One of the NCC contains *Performance Requirements* [F7P1](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f7_sound_transmission_and_insulation.md#f7p1) through to [F7P4](/ncc/2025/volume_one/f7_sound_transmission_and_insulation.md#f7p4) which cover sound transmission and insulation in walls and floors of [Class 2](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g3), [Class 3](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g4) and [Class 9c](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g10) buildings.
> - (2) [Part H4](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h4_health_and_amenity.md) in Volume Two of the NCC contains *Performance Requirement* [H4P6](/ncc/2025/volume_two/h4_health_and_amenity.md#h4p6) which covers sound insulation of walls in [Class 1](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g2) buildings.
>

<a id="d1d2"></a>

### D1D2 Undue noise

To prevent undue noise, *plumbing* and *drainage* systems must comply with—

- (a) [B1D3](/ncc/2025/volume_three/b1_cold_water_services.md#b1d3) for cold water services.
- (b) [B2D9](/ncc/2025/volume_three/b2_heated_water_services.md#b2d9) for *heated water* services.
- (c) [B3D3](/ncc/2025/volume_three/b3_non_drinking_water_services.md#b3d3) for *non-drinking water* services.
- (d) [B4D2](/ncc/2025/volume_three/b4_fire_fighting_water_services.md#b4d2) for fire-fighting water services.
- (e) [B6D2](/ncc/2025/volume_three/b6_rainwater_services.md#b6d2) for *rainwater services*.
- (f) [C1D3](/ncc/2025/volume_three/c1_sanitary_plumbing_systems.md#c1d3) for sanitary *plumbing* systems.
- (g) [C2D4](/ncc/2025/volume_three/c2_sanitary_drainage_systems.md#c2d4) for sanitary *drainage* systems.

<a id="d1d3"></a>

### D1D3 Sound insulation

> **NT variation**: NT deletes D1D3 Sound insulation.
>
> This clause has deliberately been left blank.
>

(1) If a *plumbing* or *drainage* system serves or passes through more than one *sole-occupancy unit* in a [Class 2](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g3), [Class 3](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g4) or [Class 9c](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g10) building, it must be separated from the rooms of any other *sole-occupancy unit* by construction with an R<sub>w</sub> + C<sub>tr </sub> (airborne) not less than—

- (a) 40 if the adjacent room is a *habitable room* (other than a kitchen); or
- (b) 25 if the adjacent room is a kitchen or a non-*habitable room*.

(2) In [Class 2](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g3), [Class 3](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g4) or [Class 9c](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g10) buildings, a flexible coupling must be used to connect a pump to a *plumbing* or *drainage* system.

(3) If a *plumbing* or *drainage* system is located in (or passes through) a *separating wall* in a [Class 1](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g2) building, it must—

- (a) only be installed in *discontinuous construction*; and
- (b) not be fixed to the wall of a dwelling that it does not serve; and
- (c) have a gap not less than 10 mm between the pipe and a wall of a dwelling that it does not serve; and
- (d) not be chased in if the *separating wall* is made of concrete or masonry.

> **Explanatory**
>
> [Specification 28](/ncc/2025/volume_one/specification_28_sound_insulation_for_building_elements.md) lists sound ratings for some common forms of construction. If any of these common forms of construction are used, the *plumbing* or *drainage* system must be installed in accordance with the requirements of that Specification.
>

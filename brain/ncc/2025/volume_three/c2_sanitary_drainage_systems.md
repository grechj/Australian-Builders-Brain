From **NCC 2025 Volume Three**, Sanitary plumbing and drainage systems.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (NSW, NT, SA, TAS, VIC, WA) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This Part sets out the requirements for any part of a sanitary *drainage* system of a property including from sanitary fixtures, appliances and sanitary *plumbing* systems from the *point of connection* to an *approved disposal system*.

## Objectives

<a id="c2o1"></a>

### C2O1 Objective

The Objective of this Part is to—

- (a) safeguard people from illness, injury or *loss* (including *loss* of *amenity*) due to the failure of a sanitary *drainage* installation; and
- (b) ensure that a sanitary *drainage* installation is suitable; and
- (c) conserve water and energy; and
- (d) safeguard the environment; and
- (e) safeguard public and private infrastructure; and
- (f) safeguard people from illness caused by the discharge of *swimming pool* waste water; and
- (g) protect *other property* from damage caused by the discharge of *swimming pool* waste water; and
- (h) ensure that a sanitary *drainage* installation is designed and is capable of being maintained so that throughout its serviceable life it will continue to satisfy Objectives (a) to (g).

## Functional Statements

<a id="c2f1"></a>

### C2F1 Disposal system

Sanitary fixtures and sanitary appliances must be provided with an adequate disposal system that does not impact adversely on occupants of the premises, property, the environment or the *Network Utility Operator’s* infrastructure.

<a id="c2f2"></a>

### C2F2 Swimming pool wastewater disposal

Adequate means for the disposal of *swimming pool* water and *drainage* is to be provided for each *swimming pool*.

## Performance Requirements

<a id="c2p1"></a>

### C2P1 Disposal

Sewage must be conveyed through a sanitary *drainage* system to an *approved disposal system* authorised by an authority having jurisdiction.

> **Explanatory**
>
> An *approved disposal system* may be—
>
> - (1) an *on-site wastewater management system*; or
> - (2) a connection to a *Network Utility Operator's* sewerage system.
>

<a id="c2p2"></a>

### C2P2 Swimming pool drainage

> **NT variation**: NT deletes C2P2 Swimming pool drainage.
>
> This clause has deliberately been left blank.
>

(1) An adequate means of draining a *swimming pool* must be designed and installed in a way that will not cause illness to people.

(2) An adequate means of draining a *swimming pool* must be designed and installed in a way that will not affect any *other property*.

<a id="c2p3"></a>

### C2P3 Access

Access must be provided to components of a sanitary *drainage* system that require maintenance or replacement including mechanical devices, controls and for clearing *blockages*.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Access to mechanical components may require removal of an access panel, cover, door, or similar obstruction.
>

<a id="c2p4"></a>

### C2P4 Ventilation

(1) A sanitary *drainage* system must ensure there is adequate ventilation to avoid foul air and gases accumulating in the sanitary *drainage* and sewerage system.

(2) A sanitary *drainage* system must ensure that ventilation is provided to avoid hydraulic load imbalance such that—

- (a) there is less than a 1% likelihood during the peak hour that when any fixture discharges, air pressure at any trap seal exceeds ±375 Pa difference from atmospheric pressure; or
- (b) an *equivalent* level of safety to human health is achieved as a system complying to (a).

<a id="c2p5"></a>

### C2P5 Contamination

(1) The entry of water, foul gases, sewerage and sullage from a sanitary *drainage system* into buildings must be avoided.

(2) A sanitary *drainage system* must be protected against internal contamination.

(3) The entry of foul gases from a sanitary *drainage system* into buildings must be avoided, such that—

- (a) at pressures of up to ±375 Pa, water trap seals will not be reduced to depths less than—
  - (1) 70 mm for trap seals in pressurised rooms; and
  - (2) 25 mm for all other applications; or
- (b) an equivalent level of safety to human health is achieved as a system complying to (a).

(4) The entry of *surface water*, *sub-surface water* and stormwater into a sanitary *drainage system* must be avoided.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Consideration should be given to the type, quality and quantity of waste being discharged to a sanitary *drainage system* and the suitability and compatibility of the materials used.
>

<a id="c2p6"></a>

### C2P6 Uncontrolled discharge

Any failure or *uncontrolled discharge* from a sanitary *drainage system* must be avoided.

## Verification Methods

<a id="c2v1"></a>

### C2V1 Velocity and liquid-to-air ratio

Compliance with [C2P1](#c2p1) is achieved if the sanitary *drainage* system is designed to operate with—

- (a) a liquid-to-air ratio of between 1 to 1 and 0.65 to 0.35; and
- (b) a minimum velocity of 0.8 m/s; and
- (c) a maximum velocity of—
  - (1) 2 m/s under normal operating conditions; and
  - (2) 3.5 m/s under surge conditions.

> **Explanatory**
>
> - The purpose of the minimum velocity is to minimise the likelihood of *blockage* in the sanitary *drainage* system.
> - The maximum velocity is intended to minimise the likelihood of damage to the system.
>

<a id="c2v2"></a>

### C2V2 Pressure testing

Compliance with [C2P1](#c2p1) is achieved if the sanitary *drainage* system passes one or more of the pressure tests set out in AS/NZS 3500.2.

<a id="c2v3"></a>

### C2V3 Determination of sanitary drainage wastewater flowrates

(1) Compliance with [C2P1](#c2p1) for pipe sizing is verified for each sanitary *drainage* pipework section when the design flow rate is not less than—

- (a) the probable simultaneous wastewater flow rate calculated in accordance with (2); or
- (b) the Discharge Unit (DU) value of the highest fixture connected upstream of the pipework section as given by [Table C2V3b](#table_6ef5eff1), in litres per second, whichever is greater.

(2) For the purposes of (1)(a), for each pipework section, the design probable simultaneous wastewater flow rate must be calculated in accordance with the following:

`QTotal=K∑DU+QOther`

(3) In the equation shown at (2):

- (a) QTotal = the probable simultaneous wastewater flowrate for that pipework section (l/s).
- (b) QOther = the sum of any other wastewater flowrates such as pumped discharges (l/s).
- (c) K = the frequency factor given in [Table C2V3a](#table_22bea7f1) (dimensionless).
- (d) ∑DU = the sum of the discharge units as given in [Table C2V3b](#table_6ef5eff1) connected upstream of that pipework section (dimensionless).

### C2V4 Sanitary drainage design

(1) Compliance with [C2P1](#c2p1) for main drains and branch drains is verified if (2) to (4) are complied with.

(2) Sanitary *drainage* pipework shall be sized in accordance with AS 2200 and [C2V1](#c2v1).

(3) The following limitations shall be applied:

- (a) The size of the drain shall be of nominal size as specified in Table 3.4.1 of AS/NZS 3500.2.
- (b) The minimum size of a main drain shall be as per Clause 3.3.2 of AS/NZS 3500.2.
- (c) The minimum size of a branch drain shall be as per Clause 3.3.3 of AS/NZS 3500.2.
- (d) Not more than 2 water closet pans shall be connected to a vented DN 80 branch drain.

(4) When using the Colebrook-White equation provided in AS 2200—

- (a) minimum and maximum pipe grades shall be designed such that (2) is satisfied; and
- (b) the filling degree shall be designed such that (2) is satisfied; and
- (c) the flowrate of the designed drain must be greater than or equal to that determined in accordance with [C2V3](#c2v3); and
- (d) unless alternative values are appropriate, the design values must be—
  - (1) 1.5 mm for pipe roughness; and
  - (2) 1.01 x 10<sup>-6</sup> m<sup>2</sup>s<sup>-1</sup> for kinematic viscosity.

### C2D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

(1) Where a *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution* is proposed, *Performance Requirements* [C2P1](#c2p1) to [C2P7](#c2p7) are satisfied by complying with [C2D2](#c2d2) to [C2D5](#c2d5).

(2) Where a *Performance Solution* is proposed, the relevant *Performance Requirements* must be determined in accordance with [A2G2(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g2) and [A2G4(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g4) as applicable.

### C2D2 Invert levels

The invert level of a trap or gully weir must be a minimum of 10 mm higher than the soffit of the pipe to which it connects.

<a id="c2d3"></a>

### C2D3 Swimming pool drainage

Where pumped discharge from a *swimming pool* discharges into a sanitary *drainage system* it must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.2.

> **NT variation**: NT replaces subclause (1).
>
> In Northern Territory, discharging water from swimming pools into sanitary drainage is not allowed.
>

> **Explanatory**
>
> Consideration should be given to requirements of the relevant *Network Utility Operator*. Where a *Network Utility Operator* does not permit *swimming pool* discharge to the sanitary *drainage* system, a *Performance Solution* will be necessary to demonstrate the suitability of the alternative drainage method.
>

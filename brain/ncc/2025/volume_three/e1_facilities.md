From **NCC 2025 Volume Three**, Facilities and ancillary additions.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). 

This Part sets out the requirements to allow people with disability to use the facilities provided by a *plumbing* and *drainage* system.

## Objectives

<a id="e1o1"></a>

### E1O1 Objective

The Objective of this Part is to—

- (a) ensure that the facility of a *plumbing* and *drainage* system (including an installation provided for use by people with disability) is suitable; and
- (b) ensure that the facility of a *plumbing* and *drainage* system installation throughout its serviceable life will continue to satisfy the requirements of Objective (a).

## Functional Statements

<a id="e1f1"></a>

### E1F1 Facilities

The facility of a *plumbing* and *drainage* system must be designed and installed so as to be operable by people with disability.

## Performance Requirements

<a id="e1p1"></a>

### E1P1 Facilities for people with disability

Where a *plumbing* and *drainage* system is provided, supply taps or other operational controls must be *accessible* and suitable for use.

<a id="e1d1"></a>

### E1D1 Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

(1) Where a *Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution* is proposed, *Performance Requirement* [E1P1](#e1p1) is satisfied by complying with [E1D2](#e1d2).

(2) Where a *Performance Solution* is proposed, the relevant *Performance Requirements* must be determined in accordance with [A2G2(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g2) and [A2G4(3)](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a2_compliance_with_the_ncc.md#a2g4) as applicable.

<a id="e1d2"></a>

### E1D2 General requirements

Where supply taps or other operational controls are provided in sanitary facilities for people with disability they must be in accordance with—

- (a) AS 1428.1 (2001) and AS 1428.2 for passenger use areas of [Class 9b](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g10) and [Class 10](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g11) public transport buildings; and
- (b) AS 1428.1 (2021) for all other buildings.

From **NCC 2025 Volume Three**, Water services.[^abcb_ncc] Defined terms are in *italics*; see [definitions](definitions.md). State and territory variations (NSW, SA, VIC) are shown in quotes beside the clause they change.

This Part sets out the requirements for any part of a *rainwater service* from the *point of connection* of the *rainwater storage*, to the rainwater points of discharge.

This Part applies to a *rainwater service* supplied by stored rainwater collected from a roof, where a separate cold water service is provided for drinking and personal hygiene from a *Network Utility Operator's* supply.

This Part does not apply to rainwater used only for irrigation purposes.

Where water provided by a *Network Utility Operator* is not available or not utilised as the primary water source for drinking and personal hygiene, roof collected rainwater is considered to be a cold water service supplied by an alternative source.

## Objectives

<a id="b6o1"></a>

### B6O1 Objective

The Objective of this Part is to—

- (a) safeguard people from illness, injury or *loss* (including *loss* of *amenity*) due to the failure of a *rainwater service*; and
- (b) conserve water; and
- (c) safeguard the environment; and
- (d) safeguard public and private infrastructure; and
- (e) ensure that a *rainwater service* installation throughout its serviceable life will continue to satisfy the requirements of Objectives (a) to (d).

## Functional Statements

<a id="b6f1"></a>

### B6F1 Rainwater service

A *rainwater service* must be designed and installed so as to operate in a way that avoids the likelihood of inadvertent contamination of any *drinking water* service and minimises any adverse impact on building occupants, the *Network Utility Operator’s* infrastructure, property and the environment.

## Performance Requirements

<a id="b6p1"></a>

### B6P1 Pressure

The points of discharge of a *rainwater service* must—

- (a) have—
  - (1) a working pressure of not less than 50 kPa; and
  - (2) a static pressure within a building of not more than 500 kPa; or
- (b) have water pressures suitable for the correct functioning of the fixture or appliance where water pressures outside of (a)(i) and (a)(ii) are required.

<a id="b6p2"></a>

### B6P2 Velocity

Water velocity within the *rainwater service* pipework must not exceed 3 m/s for no less than 99% of the time that water is required during the peak hour.

> **Explanatory**
>
> During the hour of heaviest usage in reticulated *rainwater service* pipework, a velocity of 3 m/s must not be exceeded for 99% of the time that any downstream fixture is in use.
>

<a id="b6p4"></a>

### B6P4 Access

Access must be provided to components of a *rainwater service* that require maintenance or replacement, including mechanical devices and controls.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Access to mechanical components may require removal of an access panel, cover, door or similar obstruction.
>

<a id="b6p5"></a>

### B6P5 Isolation

Means of isolation must be provided to a *rainwater service* and components that require testing, maintenance, or replacement, including mechanical devices, appliances and controls.

> **Explanatory**
>
> Isolation valves are a means of providing isolation.
>

<a id="b6p6"></a>

### B6P6 Identification

Pipes and pipe outlets that form part of a *rainwater service* must be clearly identified.

<a id="b6p7"></a>

### B6P7 Uncontrolled discharge

The likelihood of failure of, or *uncontrolled discharge* from, a *rainwater service* must be avoided.

<a id="b6p8"></a>

> **VIC variation**: VIC adds B6P8 Rainwater service.
>
> A *rainwater service* must—
>
> - (a) avoid the likelihood of contamination of water within the *rainwater service*, the cold water service, or the *Network Utility Operator's* *drinking water* supply (if connected); and
> - (b) not pose a hazard to public health; and
> - (c) not have an unprotected *cross-connection* with any *Network Utility Operator's* *drinking water* service; and
> - (d) ensure continual supply of water for sanitary flushing.
>
> > **Application**
> >
> > B6P8(d) applies if a reticulated water supply from a network utility operator is connected, or proposed to be connected, to a building where a rainwater tank is installed for the purpose of sanitary flushing.
> >
>

### B6D2 General requirements

> **SA variation**: SA replaces B6D2 General requirements.
>
> A *rainwater service* must comply with Section 15 of AS/NZS 3500.1 with the following variation—
>
> (d) For [Class 1](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g2) buildings and extensions or additions to [Class 1](/ncc/2025/volume_two/a6_building_classification.md#a6g2) buildings the following applies.

### B6D4 Water efficiency

> **NSW variation**: NSW replaces B6D4 Water Efficiency.
>

>
> - (a) Have a dual flushing mechanism; and
> - (b) Be a minimum 3 Star WELS rating discharging not more than 6 litres or 4.5 litre for a full flush.
>

>
> - (a) Be a minimum 2 Star WELS rating discharging a volume of not more than 2.5 litres of each—

>   - (2) 600mm length of continuous urinal wall; and
> - (b) Not be set-cycled or activated by any method other than manual or use activation.

(1) Cisterns or flushing devices for water closet pans must—

- (a) be a minimum of 3 Star WELS rating, discharging not more than 6 litres or 4.5 litres for a full flush; and
- (b) have a dual flushing mechanism.

(2) Cisterns or flushing devices for urinals must—

- (a) be a minimum of 2 Stars WELS rating, discharging not more than 2.5 litres; and
- (b) serve a single stall or 600 mm length of continuous urinal wall; and
- (c) not be automatic or set-cycle.

> **Application**

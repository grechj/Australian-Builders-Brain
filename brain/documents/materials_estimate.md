# Structure

Use this outline to produce the document in any format (your own letterhead, an email, a PDF). To fill the template itself, use `tools/fill_template.py`.

**Sheet: Setup**

- Fields: Business Name, ABN, Builder Licence, Phone, Email, Project Name, Project Address, Client Name, Job Description, Pricing Source, Store / Supplier, Prices Checked On, ROM Low (%), ROM High (%)

**Sheet: Materials**

- One row per entry from row 5. Columns to enter: Item, Specification, Qty Needed, Unit, Pack Size, Product / Item No., Unit Price inc GST ($), Source, Priced On, Notes
- Calculated columns: Packs to Buy, Line Total inc GST ($)

**Sheet: Summary**

- Lines: ESTIMATE SUMMARY, Project, Job, Prices from, Prices checked on, Materials total (inc GST), GST included, Materials total (ex GST), Items priced, Items NOT priced (not in total), ROM low (inc GST), ROM high (inc GST), A rough order of magnitude (ROM) for budgeting only. Read the Qualifications sheet before using these figures.

**Sheet: Qualifications**

- Lines: QUALIFICATIONS, 1. Bunnings prices are used as a guide only. They are retail prices including GST, for the store and date shown, and change over time and between stores., 2. Specialist suppliers (timber yards, plasterboard, tile and paint suppliers, concrete plants, trade accounts) often price better for larger quantities and may supply better grades or products. Get trade quotes before committing., 3. Quantities are a takeoff estimate using rules of thumb (spacings, coverage, waste). Check them against the drawings and the manufacturers' guides., 4. Excludes labour, delivery, equipment hire, waste removal, fees, engineering and contingency unless listed above., 5. Structural member sizes, spans and fixings must be confirmed from AS 1684, the supplier's span tables or an engineer. Products must suit the use (for example H3 or H4 treatment outdoors) and comply with the NCC., 6. Items marked NOT priced are not in the total., 7. The ROM range (default -15% / +30%) is for budgeting and deciding whether to proceed. It is not a quote or a fixed price.

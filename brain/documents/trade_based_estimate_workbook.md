**Sheet: Setup**

- Fields: Business Name, ABN, Builder Licence, Address, Phone, Email, Default Hourly Rate, Default Daily Rate, Default Markup on Cost (%), Default Contingency (%), GST Rate

- One row per entry from row 9. Columns to enter: Trade/Task, Description, Qty, Unit, Notes

# Materials

Takeoff jobs: `electrical_rough_in` ([materials takeoff](/computations/materials_takeoff.md)).

Typical materials:

- TPS cable (sizes per circuit design)
- Conduit and fittings
- Switchboard, RCBOs and circuit breakers
- Power points and switches
- Light fittings and downlights (IC-rated where insulation abuts)
- Interconnected smoke alarms
- Data cable and outlets
- Mounting brackets, wall plates, cable clips

Where to price them (beyond the Bunnings retail guide):

- Electrical wholesalers (for example Rexel, L&H, CNW, Middy's)
- Bunnings, Mitre 10 and independent hardware (Home Timber & Hardware and similar) for general items

# Key standards and rules (referenced, not reproduced)

- AS/NZS 3000 (Wiring Rules)
- AS/NZS 3008 (cable selection)
- AS 3786 (smoke alarms)
- AS/NZS 4777 and AS/NZS 5139 (solar inverters and batteries)

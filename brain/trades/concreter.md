# Materials

Takeoff jobs: `concrete_slab` ([materials takeoff](/computations/materials_takeoff.md)).

Typical materials:

- Ready-mix concrete (strength class per design)
- Reinforcing mesh and bar
- Bar chairs and trench mesh supports
- Vapour barrier and slab-edge termite protection
- Formwork timber and pegs
- Expansion joint and sealant
- Curing compound

Where to price them (beyond the Bunnings retail guide):

- Concrete batching plants (ready-mix)
- Steel reinforcement suppliers
- Bunnings, Mitre 10 and independent hardware (Home Timber & Hardware and similar) for general items

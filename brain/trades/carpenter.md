# Materials

Takeoff jobs: `timber_deck`, `stud_wall`, `paling_fence` ([materials takeoff](/computations/materials_takeoff.md)).

Typical materials:

- Framing timber (MGP10/MGP12, treated to suit)
- Engineered beams and LVL
- Trusses and wall frames (prefabricated)
- Bracing and tie-down (straps, rods, framing anchors)
- Nails, screws, bolts, framing brackets
- Decking, joists, bearers, posts, stirrups
- Doors, jambs, architraves, skirting
- Sarking and wall wrap

Where to price them (beyond the Bunnings retail guide):

- Timber yards and frame and truss plants
- Bunnings, Mitre 10 and independent hardware (Home Timber & Hardware and similar) for general items

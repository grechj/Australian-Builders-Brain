# Materials

Takeoff jobs: `plumbing_rough_in` ([materials takeoff](/computations/materials_takeoff.md)).

Typical materials:

- Copper and PEX pipe and fittings
- PVC drainage pipe and fittings
- Valves (tempering, isolation, backflow)
- Hot water units
- Tapware and fixtures (supply or fit-off)
- Stormwater pits and grates
- Gas pipe and fittings
- Pipe lagging

Where to price them (beyond the Bunnings retail guide):

- Plumbing merchants (for example Reece, Tradelink)
- Bunnings, Mitre 10 and independent hardware (Home Timber & Hardware and similar) for general items
